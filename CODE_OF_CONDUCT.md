# Code of Conduct

The `anneal` project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (v2.1).

All participants in the project's spaces — issues, pull requests, and
discussions — are expected to uphold it. Instances of abusive, harassing, or
otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at
rgoswami@ieee.org. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly.

See the rendered [Code of Conduct](https://anneal.rgoswami.me/code_of_conduct.html)
in the documentation for the full text.
