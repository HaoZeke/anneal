# Predictions before the scored dynamic-lattice runs

Written before any run of seeds 3000-3029.

The search is the dynamic lattice method of Shao, Cheng and
Cai, J. Comput. Chem. 25, 1693 (2004), with Table 1 counts
for this size and the lowest-index tie cut. The budget is
2000000 charged evaluations.

Predicted global-minimum hits: 10 of 30.
The Wilson 95% interval on 10/30 is 0.192 to 0.512,
which is 6 to 15 hits.

The uniform tie cut uses the same search and breaks both
selection cuts by a uniform draw. Predicted hits: 10 of 30,
with the same Wilson interval, 6 to 15.

A hit is a re-quenched energy at or below -582.086641.
The re-quench asks for a gradient infinity norm of 1e-8.
These minima have stopped near a few times 1e-6, and a
further line search has not lowered that norm. The hit
test is the energy.

No comparison with the reference program is claimed here.
Those seeds have not been run.
