# The structures and maps of fig:systems, fig:landscapes, fig:disconnectivity and fig:si-structures to fig:si-water.
# structures/ is copied byte for byte from tests/fixtures/ of anneal at anneal_commit; the
# test of anneal named by each check holds it to its reference energy. landfold/ is the
# output of the occupancy_landfold_book example of that commit on the dumps each map names,
# run from the folder of those dumps with its stdout to the json:
#   occupancy_landfold_book --label LJ<size> --dump-hist <hist> <inputs> > <json>
# The water folds are the same command on the hexamer dumps of each engine; they collapse,
# since the packing book reads every atom alike, and no map draws them.
# ccd/ is the Cambridge Cluster Database at the paths of its URLs, ccd/TIP4P/ under ccd_tip4p
# and the rest under ccd_base, fetched on ccd_fetched; references.csv gives the published energy of each
# reference structure as the table of the database at the URL of its row prints it, and
# system_visuals.py holds each structure to it by the check of its class.
# sketchmap/ holds the reference minima that campaign_landscape.py of the paper at
# sketchmap_of_record pinned, copied byte for byte from scripts/ of that commit, and the
# projection of each size that sketchmap_run writes, with the binaries of sketchmap_build.
# Since the build writes its folder into the debug information, the digests of the
# binaries hold for a build in sketchmap_build_dir.
