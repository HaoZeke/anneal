\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Seed-paired knockouts of
a basin-hopping search: structure priors, templates, stall rules, and the
length unit, with comparisons to GMIN, CREST, and the Atomic Simulation
Environment,'' as a Full Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in 2023
under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated Annealing.''
The title and the content have changed.

The manuscript asks which parts of one basin-hopping code change how many
seeds reach the global minimum.
Each part that has been run with and without it, on the same seeds from the
same starts, is reported as a paired knockout with the exact McNemar test:
the structure priors, the structure templates, a restart on a plateau, the
length unit of the preset, and the choice among the moves.
Switching off the structure priors lowers the hits on six Cu--Au and Pt--Cu
Gupta alloys, removing the structure templates lowers them on a Morse
cluster, and measuring lengths in units of the pair minimum raises them on
another; a restart on a plateau and a Thompson choice among the moves change
no cluster significantly.
Parts of the search with no such run are described as design, with no claim
about their effect.

The benchmarks follow, each as evidence for one claim.
Paired by seed, the code hits more seeds than GMIN on a Lennard-Jones
cluster and on two rigid TIP4P water clusters, and more than CREST on the
GFN2-xTB water hexamer with both codes stopped at the same number of energy
and gradient calls.
The comparisons with the Atomic Simulation Environment and AGOX GOFEE are
reported at the effort each run spent.
Every printed number is computed from rows committed to the repository.

A table of contents graphic at 3.25~in by 1.75~in accompanies the submission.
