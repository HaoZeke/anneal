\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Template-free basin
hopping with adaptive moves for funneled cluster landscapes,'' as a Full
Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in 2023
under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated Annealing.''
The title and the content have changed.

The manuscript presents a template-free basin-hopping search that combines
quenched structural moves, adaptive move allocation, basin-level memory, and
stall exits to cross funneled cluster landscapes.
The evidence is organized by claim.
On Lennard-Jones, Morse, and rigid water, this search and GMIN are each
reported at that arm's own numeric ceiling.
Removing the former structure templates costs hits on a short-ranged Morse
cluster.
Neither rescaling the preset lengths nor replacing adaptive move choice by a
uniform draw produces a detectable hit-rate change.
The anneal octamer rows record no target, tolerance, budget, or stop.
anneal's rows do not record the CREST target or budget.
There is no paired test, and the Holm family for CREST is empty.
The independently started comparisons with the Atomic
Simulation Environment share one numeric call ceiling and wall cap
within a cluster.
They are reported descriptively because the starts are unpaired and
runs reaching the shared cap are censored, so no cross-arm
significance test is reported.
Every printed number is computed from rows committed to the repository.

A table of contents graphic at 3.25~in by 1.75~in accompanies the submission.
The corresponding author is named above.
