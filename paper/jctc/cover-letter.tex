\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Removing the structure
templates of a basin-hopping search: a seed-paired knockout, with comparisons
to GMIN and the Atomic Simulation Environment,'' as a Full Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in 2023
under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated Annealing.''
The title and the content have changed.

The manuscript asks whether one part of a basin-hopping code changes how
many seeds reach the global minimum.
The structure templates, run with and without them at a revision and its
child in the history of the code, on the same seeds from the same starts, are
reported as a paired knockout with the exact McNemar test: removing them
lowers the hits on a Morse cluster.
Parts of the search with no such run are described as design, with no claim
about their effect.

The benchmarks follow, each as evidence for one claim.
Paired by seed, the code hits more seeds than GMIN on a Lennard-Jones
cluster, on two Morse clusters, and on two rigid TIP4P water clusters.
The comparisons with the Atomic Simulation Environment are
reported at the effort each run spent.
Every printed number is computed from rows committed to the repository.

A table of contents graphic at 3.25~in by 1.75~in accompanies the submission.
The suggested reviewers are listed in the submission form.
