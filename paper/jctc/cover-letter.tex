\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``One basin-hopping setting for
Lennard-Jones, Morse, gold, and water clusters,'' as a Full Article.

We present one setting for atomic and rigid-molecule clusters whose moves,
temperature, and move choice do not change with size: six moves, a reduced
temperature of \(0.8\), and a depth posterior that chooses the move.
Only the quench changes, at 98 atoms, and structure priors propose
close-packed motifs.
The manuscript does not claim a new move.
The claim is the combination, in which one normal-gamma posterior per move
chooses among six moves from the depth each reaches and a stall never restarts
the walker at random, and the way the combination is tested.

Against GMIN on Lennard-Jones clusters, this setting hits
more seeds on 6 of 8 and GMIN hits more on one.
On nine Sutton--Chen clusters, this setting hits at least as many seeds as
GMIN on each, while GMIN has the lower median wall on seven.
The losses and the ties stay in the text and in the tables.

The manuscript also reports TIP4P clusters against GMIN.

Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation publishes software for molecular
simulation.
This article belongs in that journal because committed scripts generate the
GMIN, ASE, GOFEE, and Sutton--Chen tables from committed row or seed files and
the TIP4P table from its committed per-seed rows.
