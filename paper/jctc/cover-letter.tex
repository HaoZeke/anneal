\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Basin hopping for atomic clusters with a fixed move library,'' as an Article.

This submission replaces arXiv:2302.02811, ``Annealing chains that communicate: low-energy cluster structures across engines with one configuration.''
The move library, the temperature and the hit rule stay fixed.
The surface list changes once, at 98 atoms: below that size the relaxation may add a diameter penalty at 0.7 of the largest pair distance, and a stall does not redraw the walker.
On six shared starts the search hits 6, 6, 4, 5 and 6 of LJ\(_{38}\), LJ\(_{55}\), LJ\(_{75}\), LJ\(_{98}\) and LJ\(_{104}\).
GMIN hits 3, 6, 0, 0 and 2.
At LJ\(_{75}\) the GMIN median wall is shorter and GMIN records no hits.
An earlier campaign records 36 of 64 hits on LJ\(_{98}\) against 61 of 64 for GMIN, and no hits on TIP4P clusters of 15 and 20 molecules where GMIN hits.
On the water hexamer the polished pairs are one minimum, inside or at the vtight threshold, reached at a lower call count than CREST's energy-and-gradient totals.
On \((\mathrm{H_2O})_{20}\) with native xtb the search hits 3 of 6 seeds and CREST hits 1 of 6, and the search spends more wall time.
