\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Template-free basin
hopping with adaptive moves for funnelled cluster landscapes,'' as a Full
Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in 2023
under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated Annealing.''
The title and the content have changed.

The manuscript presents a template-free basin-hopping search that combines
quenched structural moves, adaptive move allocation, basin-level memory, and
stall exits to cross funnelled cluster landscapes.
The evidence is organized by claim.
At arm-specific work definitions the search reaches Lennard-Jones, Morse,
and rigid-water minima that trap the corresponding GMIN arms.
Removing the former structure templates lowers M\(_{38}\) hits at
\(\rho=14\) from 12 to 4 of 12
\((p=7.8\times 10^{-3})\); across every intervening code change, LJ\(_{75}\)
hits fall from 12 to 5 of 12.
At 12 seeds, neither rescaling the preset lengths nor replacing adaptive
move choice by a uniform draw produces a detectable hit-rate change in the
tested panels.
The independently started, wall-capped comparisons with the Atomic
Simulation Environment are reported descriptively because their arms are
censored and do not share starts.
Every printed number is computed from rows committed to the repository.

A table of contents graphic at 3.25~in by 1.75~in accompanies the submission.
