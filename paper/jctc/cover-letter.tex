\noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``One basin-hopping setting for
Lennard-Jones, Morse, gold, and water clusters,'' as a Full Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in 2023
under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated Annealing.''
The title and the content have changed.
The manuscript now reports one basin-hopping setting and the search machinery
it runs on, and it compares that setting with GMIN, with the Atomic
Simulation Environment, with AGOX GOFEE, and with CREST.

The manuscript reports one basin-hopping setting for atomic and rigid-molecule
clusters.
It has a fixed library of six moves, a reduced temperature of \(0.8\), and a
depth posterior that chooses the move, and none of the three changes with the
size of the cluster.
The manuscript does not claim a new move.
The claim is the combination, in which one normal-gamma posterior per move
chooses among six moves from the depth each reaches and a stall never restarts
the walker at random, and the way the combination is tested.
The preset also carries structure priors.

Against the Wales-group program GMIN, on seeds 2000--2011 of thirteen Lennard-Jones, Gupta, and Morse clusters in one session, this setting hits more seeds on 9 of 13, the two tie on 2, and GMIN hits more on 2.~\rerun{5}
Fisher's test on hits is below 0.05 on 6 clusters: 5 for this setting, LJ\(_{38}\), LJ\(_{75}\), LJ\(_{98}\), and M\(_{55}\) and M\(_{80}\) at \(\rho=14\), and M\(_{80}\) at \(\rho=6\) for GMIN.~\rerun{5}
GMIN hits LJ\(_{150}\) on 9 of 12 against 5 of 12, with \(p=0.21\), and M\(_{80}\) at \(\rho=6\) on 12 of 12 against 2 of 12, with \(p=6.7\times 10^{-5}\).~\rerun{5}
The median wall of a run is 0.075~s for this setting against 3.658~s for GMIN on LJ\(_{38}\), and 27.019~s against 119.9~s on LJ\(_{75}\).~\rerun{5}
On M\(_{80}\) at \(\rho=6\) it is 168.9~s against 10.691~s.~\rerun{5}
The losses and the ties stay in the text and in the tables.

The molecular test is the water hexamer at the GFN2-xTB model, and the
manuscript also reports TIP4P clusters against GMIN.
On the water hexamer the motif match hits 121 of 192 seeds for the default hop and 94 of 192 for plain CREST, both at GFN2-xTB through tblite 0.7.0, with CREST 3.0.2; two-sided Fisher's \(p=0.00744\).~\rerun{7}
The six CREST rows are single draws from one start geometry, and the text
does not read them as a success rate.

Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation publishes software for molecular
simulation.
This article belongs in that journal because the tables of the GMIN, ASE, GOFEE, and
TIP4P comparisons are generated from the committed rows by committed scripts.
