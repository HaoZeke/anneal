\vspace{1em} \noindent Dear Editor,

We ask you to consider the enclosed manuscript, ``Basin hopping on
Lennard-Jones clusters with one preset at every size,'' as a Full
Article.

This submission replaces the preprint arXiv:2302.02811, deposited in
2023 under the title ``Unified Software Design Patterns for Simulated
Annealing.''
The title has changed.
The manuscript now reports one basin-hopping preset.
That preset keeps the move library, the temperature, the census and the
orbit completion fixed at every size.
Below 98 atoms it adds a diameter penalty, and restart on stall stays
off.
The novelty is that fixed core, with the depth posterior choosing the
move.
On the water hexamer, the six crest rows are single draws from the
conformer-rotamer ensemble sampling tool (CREST).
Those rows are not a success rate.
Five polished gaps lie within \(1\times 10^{-7}\)~Eh, and seed 1 lies
above that line.
The comparison sets the 4000-call arm against CREST energy and gradient
calls, which run from 142110 to 209931.

One constructor states the size switch and the stall rule that the code
runs.
A referee can check each hexamer gap against the force criterion and
against the vtight threshold in the text.
Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation publishes software for
molecular simulation.
This article belongs in that journal.
The molecular test is a water hexamer at the GFN2-xTB model, and the
software is the preset a reader can rerun.

A table of contents graphic at 3.25~in by 1.75~in accompanies the
submission.

Suggested reviewers follow.
