Symmetry completion of a new basin is back in ``recommended`` and ``communicating`` as ``Config::symmetry_completion_on_new``, with a different source of the group. ``crate::detected_symmetry`` reads the point group off the incumbent over every rotation order the structure can carry (``order_bound``), closes the detected operations into a finite point group, and re-seats the atoms on its site set. No group, order list, size or species is named in the preset or the code. ``Outcome::symmetry_completions`` counts the completions quenched and the energy those taken gained. ``symmetry_core_fraction`` is the fraction of points, innermost first, whose group is read.
