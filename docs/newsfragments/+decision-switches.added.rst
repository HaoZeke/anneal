``Config::walkers`` takes ``WalkerSchedule::Hyperband``, which spends the ledger as Hyperband brackets of short walkers kept by successive halving, and ``Config::arm_selection`` takes ``Thompson`` and ``DiscountedUcb``, a discounted bandit over the move kernels, the angular move, the reseed and the plateau leave. All three are off by default: against develop on the first-pass panel in ``bench/decide`` none raises the hit total without a loss.
