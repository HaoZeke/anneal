Periodic and partially periodic cells with any nonzero off-diagonal entry are binned in fractional coordinates from the internal minimum image inverse, scaled by the cell size, while non-periodic fractional axes span the actual coordinate range without wrapping. Uniformly scaled tiny cells keep their scaled inverse in `MinimumImage`, while a singular or ill conditioned cell in fractional binning, or a non-finite fractional coordinate, raises an error instead of falling back to Cartesian bins.
