Periodic and partially periodic cells with any nonzero off-diagonal entry are binned in fractional coordinates from the internal minimum image inverse, shifted to a coordinate reference and wrapped on periodic axes before projection so translation stability holds at large coordinates, while non-periodic fractional axes span the actual coordinate range without wrapping. Fractional projections are retained only as reusable update workspace, and orthorhombic cells stay on their direct Cartesian path. Uniformly scaled tiny cells keep their scaled inverse in `MinimumImage`, while a singular or ill conditioned cell in fractional binning, or a non-finite fractional coordinate, raises an error instead of falling back to Cartesian bins.
