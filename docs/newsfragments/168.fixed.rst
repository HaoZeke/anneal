After a failed restraint block, the plain-surface hold in
``SurfacePortfolio`` now ends. It lifts when the plain walk reaches a
lower minimum, or once the hold has lasted more blocks than the run had
settled when it began. The next block then goes back to the restraint
that failed. Before, one failed block held the plain surface for the
rest of the run, so a ``cluster_search`` chain left on the icosahedral
shelf at 75 points never drew the restraint again.
