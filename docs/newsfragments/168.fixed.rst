After a failed restraint block, the plain-surface hold in
``SurfacePortfolio`` now ends. It lifts when the plain walk reaches a
lower minimum, or when the hold has gone more blocks without one than
the run has ever gone between new minima. The next block then goes back
to the restraint that failed. Before, one failed block held the plain
surface for the rest of the run, so a ``cluster_search`` chain left on
the icosahedral shelf at 75 points never drew the restraint again.
