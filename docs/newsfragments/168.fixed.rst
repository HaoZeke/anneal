A restraint block that does not beat the incumbent it opened on holds
the plain surface. The chain stands again where it stood when that
incumbent was found. The first such hold ends when the plain walk
reaches a lower minimum, or once the hold has lasted more blocks than
the run had settled when it began. That one lift tries the failed
restraint again. A later failure stays on the plain surface until the
chain has been quiet for longer than its own longest quiet stretch,
and that stall tries the restraint once.

The unrestrained quench of that same proposal runs on the opening
relaxation and on a restraint that did not beat the incumbent. It
replaces the restraint only when it lowers the incumbent. A restraint
that already beat the incumbent is kept, and that quench is not paid.
A full pass that starts where a restraint screen kept the restraint
does not repeat the quench. RelaxStats counts those quenches and the
ones the chain walks.
