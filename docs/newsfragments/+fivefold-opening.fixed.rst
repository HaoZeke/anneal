A stalled search steps the five atoms of its icosahedral incumbent that carry the optimal residual of a turn by the pentagon's interior angle. The quench is kept only when the rise off that incumbent is the Mackay to anti-Mackay gap. The opening does not depend on storage order or on the number of atoms. On a derived plateau that is not icosahedral, lattice growth is proposed as well as the climb, and the chain takes it only when the quench deepens the incumbent.
