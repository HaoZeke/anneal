On a directed stall, a search whose geometry is free to move can
propose one lattice body grown from a contact tetrahedron of the
current structure. The cloud stops at as many sites as the body has
distinct 4-point frames. When that count is reached with fewer sites
than the structure has points, the stall proposes nothing. Rigid-body,
active-region and frozen configurations skip the probe. A basin is
probed only while the remaining budget can still pay for every
distinct tetrahedron the point count can present, and only once.
