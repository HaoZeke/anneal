The contact repair left a short bond alone only when it matched a single-bond length, so a double, aromatic or amide bond counted as compressed and was pulled out to the single-bond sum.
Any repair of a molecule rewrote every such bond.
A bond is now compressed only inside ``shortest_bond_length``, the triple-bond fraction (0.77) of the radius sum for heavy atoms and the whole sum for a pair with hydrogen.
It is restored to that length, and a molecule at equilibrium comes out of the repair unchanged.
