A chain no longer repeats a symmetry completion that found nothing. A completion is a function of the minimum, so when the chain enters a minimum it has already completed to nothing, the same energy to relaxation precision and the same region by the chain's own identity, the group is not read again. In 100,000 charged calls on a 104-point Lennard-Jones cluster, 106 of the 232 completions of one seed were such repeats. A chain under a shared history is unaffected, and a minimum whose completion gave a product is still completed each time it is entered, as before. A walk can differ from the one before where a repeat would have found a product by rounding alone.
