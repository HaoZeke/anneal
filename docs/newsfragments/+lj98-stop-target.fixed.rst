The LJ98 stop is the Cambridge Td global minimum, -543.665361 (Leary and
Doye, Phys. Rev. E 60, R6320, 1999). ``cluster_search`` refuses a target
within 1e-3 of the Cs icosahedral minimum -543.642957 (Deaven, Chem. Phys.
Lett. 256, 195, 1996). A recorded energy at or below the published minimum
plus the tolerance counts as a hit.
