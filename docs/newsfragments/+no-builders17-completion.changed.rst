The symmetry completion of a new basin reads its group from the core and grows the closure only through products the core carries within the detection tolerance, so a loosely detected group is cut at the largest polyhedral group on operations the structure has instead of being closed on rounding. It acts the group about the better of the core's centroid and the whole structure's, the one that carries more points onto a point within the merge radius, and seats a species on all the sites together when it has fewer clear sites than atoms. When no operation carries the species, as on a near-symmetric framework with a random decoration, it reads the group from the positions alone and seats each species on its own sites. On binary Lennard-Jones this removes the calls-to-hit gap on the 55-point cell and keeps the hits of the 38-point cells. It changes the walk with completion on and leaves it bit for bit with ``symmetry_completion_on_new`` off.
