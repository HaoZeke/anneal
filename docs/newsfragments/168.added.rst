``Ledger::keep_all_evidence()`` keeps every quench boundary and accepted
transition of a run, and ``Ledger::keep_recent_evidence(cap)`` keeps the
newest ``cap`` of each. ``Ledger::dropped_quench_boundaries`` and
``Outcome::dropped_transitions`` count what was not kept.
``RelaxStats::plain_holds`` and ``RelaxStats::hold_lifts`` count the
plain-surface holds a failed restraint block began and the holds lifted
again. ``h2h_seed`` takes the cap as an optional ninth argument.
