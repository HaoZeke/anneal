``Config::boost_on_stall`` accepts the next new minimum after a stall whatever its energy, as in GOAT's gradient boost (de Souza 2025).
A new minimum is a quenched trial whose descriptor the chain's own bias has never deposited on.
The boost therefore reads that chain's store and not a shared history.
The stall length is not GOAT's count of three, which counts GOAT's own uphill walks.
A chain is stalled once its run of trials without a new minimum reaches the longest run its own discovery rate shows about once.
That length is the leading term of Schilling's longest-run law (College Math. J. 21, 196, 1990).
``Outcome::boosts`` counts the new minima that the acceptance rule refused and the boost accepted.
``lj_cluster_search`` and ``water_tip4p`` take ``boost``, which applies on communicating chains under the Metropolis rule and under minima hopping.
``goat::StallBoost`` applies the rule outside the hop loop, and ``goat::expected_longest_run`` gives the run length.
