``Config::rigid_side_walk_hand_back`` records the lowest quench of a rigid-body chain's stall side walks and continues the walker from it when it is below the walker's own minimum, and lets the shell-turn side walk come round after each further quiet stretch instead of once per run. It is off on every preset: on (H2O)16 at 2,000,000 calls it reached the global minimum on 19 of 36 seeds against 14 without, which is not significant (Fisher p 0.34).
