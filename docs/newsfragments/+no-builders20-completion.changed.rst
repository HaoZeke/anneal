A symmetry completion that the fitted screen would refuse is now finished anyway while this run's finished completions land below the chain energy at least as often as the break-even of finishing cost over finishing cost plus the calls an ordinary hop spends per downhill step. The rate is read from the run's own completions and hops, with a posterior mean and one standard deviation under a uniform prior, so it needs no constant and a run whose completions rarely land downhill is screened as before. It changes the walk with completion on and leaves it bit for bit with ``symmetry_completion_on_new`` off.
