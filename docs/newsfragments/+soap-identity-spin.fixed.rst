A hop whose SOAP proposal is the identity and whose redraws all land on
SOAP again draws its move uniformly from the other arms, and a library with
no other arm charges the skipped hop one call. The hop used to be skipped
without a charge, so a chain whose allocator kept choosing SOAP spent ever
fewer calls per hop: trofinetide seed 4 under the molecular preset was
stopped after 74 minutes short of its 1356-call budget, and a traced rerun
had skipped 32768 hops by its 953rd call. ``Outcome::soap_fallbacks``
counts the fallbacks.
