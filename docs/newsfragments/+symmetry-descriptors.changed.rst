``DescriptorClass``. ``motif_counts`` and ``motif_class`` are
``local_pair_counts`` and ``descriptor_class``.

``SPARSE_DESCRIPTOR_PER_ATOM`` and ``DENSE_DESCRIPTOR_PER_ATOM``.
