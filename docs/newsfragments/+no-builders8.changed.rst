The closure of the operations the symmetry completion detects no longer identifies products that are only within the detection tolerance of each other. Two products are one element only when they differ by no more than the rounding of a word as long as the cap (the cap times the machine epsilon, scaled to the structure's radius), and the closure is cut at the bound of the finite subgroups of O(3), as before. A closure that does not stop is not a group the core has: identifying nearby products made every element the cut keeps a distinct operation, and the completion applied all of them. The group a core is read to have is therefore the operations detected and their near-repeats, up to the cap, where a tolerant closure folded them to a smaller group that regularises the core further.
