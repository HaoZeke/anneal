The example drivers lose the options that switched the removed builders on. ``lj_cluster_search`` no longer accepts the ``sym``, ``nosym``, ``reocc``, ``orbit``, ``psym``, ``psymnew``, ``psymall``, ``reseed``, ``selfseed``, ``learncon`` and ``gtwin`` options, and an unknown option is refused as before. ``lj_joint_optimum`` rejects the ``bh-sym`` arm with its usage error, and its ``all`` selector lists no stall-symmetry arm. ``cluster_bench`` keeps the ``twin`` move library and drops ``reseed``, ``learn`` and ``sym``. ``h2h_arm3`` accepts only the ``default`` arm: the dual-core ``arm3`` it named is refused. ``lj_ensemble_splice`` loses its reoccupation exchange. The examples ``lj_core_census`` and ``sc_arm3`` are removed. ``sc_h2h``, ``conformer_search`` and the GFN2 and covalent head-to-head examples no longer print or set orbit completion, and ``sc_h2h`` no longer prints orbit counts. The examples that started from a grown icosahedron (``ico75_hop``, ``paper_controls``, ``soap_class_diag``, ``ttt_lj38``) read the sites from ``tests/fixtures/ico_growth_<n>.xyz``.
