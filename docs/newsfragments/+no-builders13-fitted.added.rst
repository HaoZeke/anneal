``Config::symmetry_completion_fitted`` (off in every preset, and absent from the resolved configuration unless set) completes a structure on the group of its framework as ``symmetry_completion_framework`` does, with three fits that name no system. It grows the closure of the detected operations only through products the core carries within the detection tolerance, so the cut at the largest polyhedral group is spent on operations the structure has, acts the group about the better of the core's centroid and the whole structure's, and seats a species on all the sites together when it has fewer clear sites than atoms. ``detected_symmetry::complete_detected_fitted`` is the call, ``symmetrise::operation_deviation`` measures how far a structure is from any operation, and the Python cluster ``Config`` reads the switch as ``symmetry_completion_fitted``.
