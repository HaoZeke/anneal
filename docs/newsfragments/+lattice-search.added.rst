Dynamic lattice search (``methods::lattice_search``): hollow sites over
triangles and squares of the current structure, an interior-grown lattice
that carries both stackings of every facet, successive placement of the
surface around the kept interior, a Metropolis walk over occupations, and
swap relaxation until no relocation lowers a point, with the ``dls`` driver
token and restoration tests on the LJ98 and LJ104 minima.
