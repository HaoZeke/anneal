The search presets change what they run on entering a new basin and when stuck. ``Config::for_cluster`` no longer symmetrises on a stall or completes the orbit of a two-core read, and runs no symmetry step at all. ``Config::recommended`` and ``Config::communicating`` no longer do either; they run ``Config::symmetry_completion_on_new`` instead, on the core the structure itself shows. ``Config::for_cluster_dual_core`` and ``Config::with_dual_orbit_core`` are gone, and rigid-body presets (TIP4P and the water arms) leave completion off. The ``MoveLibrary`` variants that reseeded from a library are gone with the builders. Runs that fixed a seed walk differently from the previous release.
