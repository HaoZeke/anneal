A monoatomic packing search whose stall exits stop paying now mixes two
template-free morphology proposals into its hops: a twin across a dense
plane, whose rotation is one step of the order the boundary layer itself
shows, and a cluster grown from the observed coordination shell. The
escape engages once, since the last best, a stall exit has fired, a
full ``restart_patience`` of calls has passed, and ``stall_patience``
hops have gone by without an improvement. A bandit over the base
library and the two proposals then decides how often each runs.
Molecular, rigid-body, multi-species, slab and frozen systems are
untouched, and a run that never meets those conditions is unchanged.
