The commit subjects were longer than 72 columns and did not carry
a scope. Rewriting them changes history, so this is one replay.

The archived tip is refs/archive/prereg/lj104-sota-v1.3 at
835b90741211f2fd00b5b9ea68cde4cc877b31af. The replay keeps that
history reachable. Each new commit uses the old commit's tree, a
subject of at most 72 columns that starts with lj104-prereg, and a
real -0600 clock time. Author and committer are Rohit Goswami
<rgoswami@ieee.org>. The commits are unsigned. No two of them share
a second, and none falls on second :00.

The five commits that add no file are in the map: c9bac09f,
45139a21, 54818da1, d77d8eb5, and cc0587fa. Their new subjects name
the pre-registration scope. replay-map.tsv is the old-to-new map.
TREE_EQUAL.txt records that each pair of trees is the same object.

The predictions, the McNemar table, the seed sets, and the
thresholds are the blobs from the archived history. This replay
does not edit them.
