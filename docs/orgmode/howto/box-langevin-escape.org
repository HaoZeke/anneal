#+TITLE: Persistent colored-noise escape in communicating box chains

* Select the escape mechanism

The native =ensemble_hop_optimize_with_config= entry accepts the existing box
and coverage configurations, selecting the engine from the supplied gradient
capability. The gradient box ensemble accepts =BoxEscape::Langevin=.
This replaces the proposal segment while retaining the bounded quench,
minimum certificate, history, escape feedback and acceptance rule. It is a
global-minimisation proposal mechanism, not a physical-time simulation.
The default is =BoxEscape::Gaussian=, the reflected Gaussian kick.

#+begin_src rust
use anneal_core::methods::{
    BoxCoverageConfig, BoxEnsembleConfig, BoxEscape, GleEscapeConfig,
    ensemble_hop_optimize_with_config,
};
use anneal_core::methods::ensemble::HistoryMode;

let config = BoxEnsembleConfig {
    replicas: 4,
    budget: 8_000,
    history: HistoryMode::None,
    escape: BoxEscape::Langevin(GleEscapeConfig::default()),
    ..BoxEnsembleConfig::default()
};
let coverage = BoxCoverageConfig::default();
// objective supplies Objective<f64>; gradient supplies Gradient<f64>.
let result = ensemble_hop_optimize_with_config(
    &objective, Some(&gradient), 7, Some(start.view()), &config, &coverage,
);
assert_eq!(result.charged, result.n_evals + result.n_grads);
assert!(result.charged <= config.budget);
#+end_src

Coverage sharing in this configuration is independent of the disabled
minimum ledger. The native entry forwards both configurations without a
separate policy loop. Without a gradient, select Gaussian escape to run the
values-only chains. An explicit one-replica configuration retains its hop
and coverage semantics; the scalar-argument =ensemble_hop_optimize=
convenience entry selects the values-only portfolio for that case.

=GleEscapeConfig= contains the maximum number of steps per escape, the lower
frequency =omega0= of the fitted band, the requested =dt=, and the noise law.
The defaults are 16 steps, =omega0= 0.2, =dt= 0.01 and colored noise. The
effective timestep resolves the same upper band frequency for both noise
laws. These are controls, not established optimal settings for every PES.

Each chain owns its physical momentum, auxiliary momentum rows and noise
generator. Segment boundaries preserve them. The optimization temperature
uses the box temperature schedule multiplied by the square of the history
escape scale; a temperature change rescales every momentum row together.
A fresh raw gradient is paid at each occupied launch point. A quench's
projected stationarity certificate cannot substitute for that force.

Shared paid-sample history supplies a direction as well as coverage's scalar
feedback. A position update that overlaps a peer's sampled region is pushed
away in normalized free-coordinate space before its objective and raw
gradient are evaluated. The correction must increase clearance from the
held peer cloud and remain inside the box. It adds no callbacks, leaves
the persistent noise state attached to its chain, and vanishes for distant
samples or disabled sharing. The coverage radius sets its finite reach.
The sample stream includes pre-polish launches, so it does not reduce every
excursion to a minimum. It is separate from counted coverage visits.

Coverage feedback needs no minimum report. A quench returning from outside
a known descriptor region increases the existing escape scale; a trial
arriving outside every held region supplies its novelty contraction.
The Gaussian multiplier is bounded to [0.25, 4]. The Langevin multiplier
is bounded to [1, 4], so novelty relaxes a revisit boost without cooling
below the calibrated base temperature. Minimum reports supply an
authoritative update instead of a second coverage update. Initial
observations do not change the scale; zero coverage height disables these
coverage-driven changes.

The temperature scale comes from paid raw energy excursions, not the
absolute objective value. Each chain initializes its scale from the energy
drop of its initial relaxation, when positive. A Langevin escape contributes
its largest finite raw trajectory energy relative to the occupied energy;
a Gaussian or values-only escape contributes its pre-polish launch energy.
The existing =AdaptiveHeight= estimator tracks these positive gaps. One
escape supplies one sample, including a rejected escape. Without positive
gap evidence the scale is one.

Hearing, acceptance and excitation use one fixed schedule value for a funded
boundary. Its raw excursion updates the scale for the following boundary.
An additive constant in the objective therefore supplies no temperature
evidence. Near-equal quenched endpoints cannot erase the raw energy variation
that the proposal has already explored. Launch values and trajectory energies
are retained from existing callbacks; calibration adds no evaluations.

* Account for the complete search

A segment consumes one launch gradient and one gradient plus one objective
per integration step. Its length shortens to reserve two combined work
units for a terminal evaluation/gradient quench. Quench depth uses the actual residual allowance.
A replica without a funded step and quench stops; it does not silently switch
to the Gaussian control. Values-only search rejects this gradient-dependent
selection before evaluating the objective.

Each gradient chain starts with a dimension-based quench allowance. If a
quench exhausts that allowance with a finite, still-uncertified projected
gradient, the chain doubles its allowance for subsequent quenches. Numerical
failures do not supply this evidence. The polisher can finish before the
allowance is spent; every request remains capped by the residual combined
work budget. This rule applies to Gaussian and Langevin escapes and to all
history modes. It changes relaxation effort, not the certificate tolerance.

The terminal trajectory point feeds the quench even when the trajectory
visits a lower objective elsewhere. Independently, the result retains the
best finite feasible objective evaluation, including trajectory points and
quench trials. Finite feasible initial and trial search boundaries supply
coverage repulsion whether accepted, rejected or uncertified. Only certified
quenches reach minimum history. Thus search occupancy, coverage, certified
minimum visits and the returned incumbent are distinct. The
[[file:../explanation/communication.org][communication contract]] describes the independent coverage configuration.

The thermostat uses the same stepping implementation as standalone GLE.
Its boundary rule is clipping in a Cartesian box, not a Manopt retraction.
Atomic and molecular point-set constraints require their geometric path;
the box option does not supply an atomic GLE escape by itself.

* Run matched noise and communication controls

The optimizer and its primary contracts are Rust. The native regression
targets cover objective-origin invariance, coverage influence and sustained
Langevin exploration:

#+begin_src sh
cargo test --locked --test box_configured_dispatch --test box_search_contract --test box_langevin_exploration --test box_coverage_escape --test box_optional_certificate --test box_sample_repulsion --test shared_sample_ownership
#+end_src

The dispatch target checks exact native entry-versus-engine callback traces,
configuration propagation, fixed coordinates and capability rejection.
The search-contract target compares full callback traces for shifted flat
objectives under Gaussian, values-only, white and colored search. The
exploration target checks an
evaluation-matched Rastrigin exploration bound. These test the native engine
without Python bindings; Python checks cover binding conformance separately.

For scalar-white noise, keep the same configuration and select
=GleNoise::White { friction: 4.0 }= as the noise law. The comparison example
=box_noise_comparison= runs Gaussian, scalar-white and colored proposals,
each with private and shared in-process coverage. Its default comparison also
switches the minimum ledger between private and shared; the =coverage= mode
disables that ledger in both arms to isolate coverage delivery. All replicas use
seeded uniform starts matched across controls, with an independent stream
for the first replica's explicitly recorded position. Equal-coordinate starts
can make initial line searches solve a separable objective without testing
global exploration. The Rastrigin and conditioned-quadratic objectives have
nonzero values at the recorded starts.

#+begin_src sh
cargo run --locked --example box_noise_comparison -- 32 8000 4 16
cargo run --locked --example box_noise_comparison -- 32 8000 4 16 coverage 0.05 0.1
cargo run --locked --example box_noise_comparison -- 32 8000 4 16 coverage 0.05 0.1 values
#+end_src

The arguments are dimension, aggregate work budget, number of seeds and
maximum Langevin steps. The =coverage= mode additionally accepts radius,
deposit height and an optional =values= or =gradient= capability; =gradient=
is the default. Values-only mode uses the existing Gaussian/pattern-search
engine and does not spend funded search work on unused minimum certificates.
Each JSON result reports the objective and gradient
counts, best value, hop count, history observations/refusals, shared deposits,
coverage observations/publications/applied and capped foreign visits,
per-chain coverage-region counts, summed history operation seconds and elapsed
seconds. The configuration record names the comparison, coverage metric,
radius, height, well-tempering, peer weight and objective capability.
Recrossing, peer-region, novelty and bounded-scale counters separate
coverage-to-proposal actions from its conditional acceptance influence.
Sample publication, delivery, overlap, actual displacement and constrained
correction counts expose direct geometric separation independently of those
scalar feedback and acceptance measurements.
Private history size
is the largest replica-local table; shared size is one ensemble-wide table.
Those sizes are not interchangeable coverage measurements. The example
rejects =HISTORY_NNG= so its communication comparison cannot be mislabeled
as a network-transport measurement. These controls test integration and
exploration; they are not a paper-wide state-of-the-art qualification.

* Diagnose an empty history

The comparison executable's =quench= control mode measures whether the
conditioned-quadratic surface can supply the unchanged history certificate
with a larger objective-call allowance:

#+begin_src sh
cargo run --locked --example box_noise_comparison -- 8 8000 1 16 quench
#+end_src

The control uses the same seeded start for each allowance and reports actual
objective/gradient work and the maximum projected gradient. Representative
seed-zero results are:

| Objective allowance | Objectives used | Gradients used | Maximum projected gradient | History certificate |
|---------------------+-----------------+----------------+----------------------------+---------------------|
|                  24 |              24 |             21 |                    3.28594 | No                  |
|                  48 |              48 |             42 |                 0.00379649 | No                  |
|                  96 |              70 |             59 |                 7.26373e-9 | Yes                 |

The certificate threshold is =1e-3=. These are standalone relaxation
controls; their budgets are not an ensemble success-rate measurement.
