#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:nil ^:nil

* Scale the preset to the pair

=Config::recommended= is written in Lennard-Jones diameters, with the pair minimum at 2^(1/6).
A Morse pair has its minimum at 1, at every range.
On reduced units, every length in the preset is 12% too wide for Morse.

Pass the pair's own length scale instead:

#+begin_src rust
use anneal_core::methods::cluster_hopping::Config;
use anneal_core::potentials::PairPotential;

let pot = PairPotential::morse(43, 6.0);
let cfg = Config::recommended_with_scales(43, pot.kind().length_scale(), 1.0);
#+end_src

=PairKind::length_scale= is =r_min / 2^(1/6)=: 1 for Lennard-Jones and 0.891 for Morse.

* What scales

- These lengths: the step, the neighbour cutoff behind the coordination counts, the merge radius, the container and the minimum separation.
- The two-phase surface's quartic penalty, as an energy per length^4.
- The temperature, which scales with the energy.
  The Morse well depth is 1, the same as the Lennard-Jones epsilon, so the temperature keeps the preset's value.

The range rho does not enter.
At rho = 6 the Morse well has the Lennard-Jones curvature once its minimum is fitted.
