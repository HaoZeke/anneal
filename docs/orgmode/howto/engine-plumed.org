* PLUMED

=RGPOT= is a collective variable whose value is the energy of an rgpot potential
on the atoms it lists.
=BIASVALUE= adds that energy, its forces and its virial to the MD engine.

#+begin_src text
LOAD FILE=libRgpotPlumed.so
ene: RGPOT ATOMS=1-256 POTENTIAL=SuttonChen OPTIONS={preset platinum cutoff 5.0} TYPES=Pt
b: BIASVALUE ARG=ene
#+end_src

- =ATOMS= are the atoms the potential sees.
- =POTENTIAL= and =OPTIONS= are a name and its =key value= options from
  :doc:`../reference/potential_names`, the same words as LAMMPS =pair_coeff=.
  Option values are Angstrom and eV.
- =TYPES= is one element symbol or atomic number for every atom, or one per atom
  in =ATOMS= order, separated by commas: =TYPES=Cu,Ni,Ni,Cu= or
  =TYPES=29,28,28,29=.
- =NOPBC= evaluates the atoms as an isolated cluster.

** Units

Positions, the cell, the value and its derivatives are in the PLUMED units: nm
and kJ/mol by default, as in GROMACS, or whatever =UNITS= sets.
They are converted to Angstrom and eV through the PLUMED =Units= object.
The rgpot eV is =96.48533212331002 kJ/mol=.
The PLUMED =UNITS ENERGY=eV= is =96.48530749925792 kJ/mol=, so a value printed
in that unit is 2.6e-7 larger, relative, than the rgpot energy in eV.
The forces the MD engine receives do not depend on =UNITS=.
Natural units stop with an error.

=plumed driver= reads lengths in its =--length-units=, nm by default.
=UNITS= only sets PLUMED's own units.
A file in Angstrom needs =--length-units A=.

** Cells

With a periodic box, =RGPOT= passes PLUMED's cell to the potential, orthogonal
or triclinic, with the positions wrapped into it.
The potential takes the minimum image, so its cutoff must be shorter than half
of every cell width.
The box derivatives are \(-V\sigma\) from the potential's stress, so the
pressure under =BIASVALUE= includes the rgpot virial and NPT runs are correct.
A potential without a stress stops on a periodic cell with =returns no stress=
instead of returning a wrong virial.

With =NOPBC=, or with no cell, the atoms sit in a cube wider than twice their
extent plus the cutoff, so no periodic image interacts.
The box derivatives then come from the positions alone.

PLUMED 2.9 reports an error raised during a step only as
=An error happened while calculating=; =RGPOT= writes the reason to the PLUMED
log first.

** Build and tests

The shared library is =libRgpotPlumed.so=, built when pkg-config finds PLUMED
and linked with =libplumedKernel=.
=-Dwith_plumed=enabled= is an error when those headers are missing.
Without PLUMED the tests are not defined and meson prints why.

=engines/plumed/run_plumed.py= runs these meson tests through =plumed driver=:

- =plumed_driver=: the LJ dimer against =rgpot-lj=.
- =plumed_debug_forces=: =--debug-forces= finite differences of the forces for
  LJ, Sutton-Chen, Stillinger-Weber and EMT, on clusters and triclinic cells,
  and for =EAMTable= with =-Dwith_eam_table=true=.
  EMT on a periodic cell has to stop with the stress error.
- =plumed_box_derivatives=: the nine box derivatives on triclinic cells.
- =plumed_units=: one cell in nm and kJ/mol, and in Angstrom and eV.
- =plumed_api=: energy, forces and virial against rgpot itself.
  The C function is =rgpot_sutton_chen_energy_forces=.
  The Python classes are =LJPot= and =StillingerWeberSi=.
  The Python part needs =-Dwith_python=true= and otherwise prints a skip.

The numerical column of =--debug-forces= is noisy at a few 1e-5 of the largest
derivative on cells of a few hundred atoms, so the tests allow 5e-5.
A central difference with a chosen step agrees with the analytic forces to about
1e-8.
