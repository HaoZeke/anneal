Publications and reproducibility packages that use ~anneal~.

* Publications

- *Typed Component Algebras for Simulated Annealing: Mechanized Equivalences, a
  Safety-Liveness Specification, and Finite-Precision Effects* (INFORMS Journal
  on Computing, under review). The reference publication: it factors SA into
  five typed components and four composition laws, proves the four
  Generalized-SA limit reductions as mechanized symbolic identities, specifies
  the workflow class in TLA+, audits the finite-precision acceptance path, and
  benchmarks eleven single-chain and Markov-chain drivers on the CUTEst set.
  The implementation contributions -- the dimension-collapse surrogate, the
  GPU-resident device backend, and the noise-aware acceptance rule -- each
  enter a single component slot and serve every variant.

* Reproducibility

- The =anneal_repro= package regenerates every number and figure of the paper
  from source: the SymPy theorem witnesses, the TLA+ checks, the
  finite-precision experiments, and the sharded CUTEst benchmark with its
  performance, data, and Pareto profiles.

* Foundations

- ~anneal~ is built on [[https://haozeke.github.io/eindir][eindir]], which provides the
  typed primitives (the =Objective= trait, =Bounds=, and the
  dimension-collapse and Chebyshev surrogate transforms).
