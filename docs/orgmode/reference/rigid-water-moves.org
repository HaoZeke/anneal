#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:nil ^:nil

#+begin_export rst
==================
Rigid water moves
==================
#+end_export

This page lists the moves of the rigid-body library in =cluster_hopping= and their settings.
It also lists the head-to-head driver =examples/h2h_seed=.
The water presets use the transferable intermolecular potential with four points (TIP4P).

* State

A rigid cluster of =n= bodies has =6 n= coordinates.
The first =3 n= are the centres.
The last =3 n= are rotation vectors, one per body.
=Config::rigid_body_geometry= gives the site positions in the body frame and the atomic number of each site.
It must be the body frame of the objective.
=RigidBodyGeometry::new_bonded= also declares the bonds inside one body.
The anchor is the most-connected site, and a tie takes the lowest site index.
A bond list in which no site is joined to two others is refused.
An empty bond list is still a body: a water then takes its oxygen and its two hydrogens from the atomic numbers.
=Config::for_tip4p= installs the frame of =Tip4pCluster=.
For =GminTip4p=, set it to =gmin_water_geometry()=, as =examples/h2h_seed= does.

* Moves

#+begin_export rst
.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Name
     - Move
     - Setting
   * - ``rkick``
     - Shift every centre and every rotation vector by uniform steps.
     - ``translate_step`` (0.35 A) and ``rotate_step`` (0.40 rad) in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rtrans``
     - Shift every centre by a uniform step in each coordinate.
     - ``translate_step``, 0.35 angstroms in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rrot``
     - Add a uniform increment to each component of every rotation vector, then fold it into the ball of radius pi.
     - ``rotate_step``, 0.40 rad in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rtrans1``
     - Shift one centre by a uniform step. The other centres and every orientation stay fixed.
       The draw multiplies that half-width by the proposal scale (src/methods/cluster_hopping/moves.rs:2560).
     - Half-width is the larger of ``min_separation`` and ``translate_step``. ``min_separation`` is 2.70 A in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rreor``
     - Compose a uniform random rotation onto three bodies when the cluster has at least three, and onto every body when it has fewer. Centres stay fixed.
     - Offered whenever ``local`` is false. There is no step length. ``for_tip4p`` needs at least two bodies.
   * - ``rreloc``
     - Move one body onto a vacant contact at ``min_separation`` and draw a new orientation.
     - ``relocate``, on in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rcycle``
     - Reverse one directed cycle of hydrogen bonds.
       Each body on the cycle turns about its anchor.
       The stalled archive walk uses the same bonds, and a stem donor inside the derived window can close onto that cycle.
     - ``reverse_cycles``, on in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rorient``
     - Compose one orientation step onto every body.
       Centres stay put.
     - ``rotate_step``, the cap of the angle, 1.64 rad in ``with_rigid_bodies``.
#+end_export

* =MoveLibrary::RigidBody=

- =n_molecules= :: bodies in the state.
- =translate_step= :: half-width of the =rtrans= step, in length units.
- =rotate_step= :: largest =rrot= increment per component, or the cap of the =rorient= angle, in radians.
- =local= :: =true= offers =rorient= in place of =rkick=, =rtrans=, =rrot=, =rtrans1=, =rreor= and =rreloc=.
- =relocate= :: =true=, and =local= false, adds =rreloc=.
- =reverse_cycles= :: =true= adds =rcycle= when a geometry is set and =has_contact_cycles= is true
  (src/methods/cluster_hopping/moves.rs:331).
  With that arm off, the stalled archive walk still reverses a chain when that same test is true
  (src/methods/cluster_hopping/stall_bridge.rs:67).
  A quench from that walk, or from the shell walk, can lower the published minimum.
  It does not reset the main chain's stall clock, it does not pay the move allocator, and a stall escape returns to a minimum the main chain quenched.
  A rigid search prints nothing. It returns the shell entries, the archive entries, and the auxiliary records kept or withheld in =Outcome::rigid_path=, and the Python =cluster_search= puts them in its =rigid_path= dict.

* Presets

- =Config::for_tip4p=, =Config::recommended_tip4p=, =Config::communicating_tip4p= :: =rkick=, =rtrans=, =rrot=, =rtrans1=, =rreor=, =rreloc= and =rcycle=.
  =for_tip4p= needs at least two bodies.
- =Config::with_rigid_bodies= :: =rorient= only.

The Python =cluster_search= with =water_sites= uses these three TIP4P presets on the caller's sites.
It labels the three sites 8, 1 and 1, so =rcycle= is on there too.
=bonds= replaces the two default O-H contacts of that custom body.

* =rcycle= rules

- The library offers =rcycle= when =reverse_cycles= is on and =has_contact_cycles= is true
  (src/methods/cluster_hopping/moves.rs:329).
  With no declared bonds, that cycle exists only when one site has atomic number 8 and two have atomic number 1.
  A declared bond graph does not need those atomic numbers.
- A hydrogen bond is a donor site within the body's contact of another body's anchor.
  That contact is the longest anchor-to-donor bond plus the widest donor-donor separation, and it is not tighter than the cycle move's own threshold of 2.5 angstroms.
  The TIP4P span is the O-H length plus the H-H length, about 2.47 angstroms, so the shared contact on that frame is 2.5 angstroms.
  The donor is the molecule of the hydrogen.
  A stem donor whose anchor lies inside one further bond length of the closing body can join the cycle on the archive walk.
- A walk from a random donor follows a random donated bond at each step.
  It never steps straight back to the molecule it came from.
  It stops at the first molecule it has visited.
  The molecules from that visit on are the cycle, three or more of them.
- When the walk finds no cycle, the move returns the state unchanged.
  The hop loop then draws another move for that hop.
- The move has no proposal ratio.

* =examples/h2h_seed=

#+begin_example
h2h_seed <lj|morse|tip4p> <n> <rho> <budget> <seed> <arm> <stop> <prefix>
#+end_example

- =arm= :: =recommended= or =base=.
           For =tip4p= these are =Config::recommended_tip4p= and =Config::for_tip4p=.
           For atoms they are =Config::recommended= and =Config::for_cluster=.
- =rho= :: the Morse range.
           Lennard-Jones and TIP4P ignore it.
- =stop= :: an energy at which the run ends early, or =-= to spend the whole budget.
- Start :: atoms come from =random_cluster_in_radius=, drawn from =seed=.
           The radius and the separation are those of =Config::for_cluster(n)=.
           Waters come from =random_tip4p_cluster=, drawn from =seed= times the 64-bit golden ratio plus 3.
           The search itself runs on =seed=.

Outputs:

- =<prefix>.trace.csv= :: =calls,energy= for each new lowest energy, when it is at least 1e-7 lower.
- =<prefix>.xyz= :: the lowest point.
                    Atoms are =X=.
                    Waters are O, H and H, with the M site left out.
                    Their sites use the parameters of the global minimisation program (GMIN).
- Summary on standard output:
  - =calls= :: calls into the potential, capped at the budget.
  - =charged= :: calls the ledger charged.
  - =lowest= :: lowest energy over the counted calls.
  - =geometry_energy= :: the energy of the written geometry.
                         For water it is computed again from the O, H and H sites.
  - =best= :: the search's own best minimum.
  - =hops=, =basins= :: hops made and distinct basins seen.
  - =wall_seconds= :: elapsed time.
