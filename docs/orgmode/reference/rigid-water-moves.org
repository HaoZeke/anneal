#+begin_export rst
==================
Rigid water moves
==================
#+end_export

This page lists the moves of the rigid-body library in =cluster_hopping= and their settings.
It also lists the head-to-head driver =examples/h2h_seed=.
The water presets use the transferable intermolecular potential with four points (TIP4P).

* State

A rigid cluster of =n= bodies has =6 n= coordinates.
The first =3 n= are the centres.
The last =3 n= are rotation vectors, one per body.
=Config::rigid_body_geometry= gives the site positions in the body frame and the atomic number of each site.
It must be the body frame of the objective.
=Config::for_tip4p= installs the frame of =Tip4pCluster=.
For =GminTip4p=, set it to =gmin_water_geometry()=, as =examples/h2h_seed= does.

* Moves

#+begin_export rst
.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Name
     - Move
     - Setting
   * - ``rtrans``
     - Shift every centre by a uniform step in each coordinate.
     - ``translate_step``, 0.35 angstroms in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rrot``
     - Add a uniform increment to each component of every rotation vector, then fold it into the ball of radius pi.
     - ``rotate_step``, 0.40 rad in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rcycle``
     - Reverse one directed cycle of hydrogen bonds.
       Each body on the cycle turns about its oxygen.
     - ``reverse_cycles``, on in ``for_tip4p``.
   * - ``rorient``
     - Compose one orientation step onto every body.
       Centres stay put.
     - ``rotate_step``, the cap of the angle, 1.64 rad in ``with_rigid_bodies``.
#+end_export

* =MoveLibrary::RigidBody=

- =n_molecules= :: bodies in the state.
- =translate_step= :: half-width of the =rtrans= step, in length units.
- =rotate_step= :: largest =rrot= increment per component, or the cap of the =rorient= angle, in radians.
- =local= :: =true= offers =rorient= in place of =rtrans= and =rrot=.
- =reverse_cycles= :: =true= adds =rcycle=.

* Presets

- =Config::for_tip4p=, =Config::recommended_tip4p=, =Config::communicating_tip4p= :: =rtrans=, =rrot= and =rcycle=.
- =Config::with_rigid_bodies= :: =rorient= only.

The Python =cluster_search= with =water_sites= uses these three TIP4P presets on the caller's sites.
It labels the three sites 8, 1 and 1, so =rcycle= is on there too.

* =rcycle= rules

- The geometry must have exactly one site of atomic number 8 and two of atomic number 1.
  Otherwise the library does not offer =rcycle=.
- A hydrogen bond is an O to H distance under 2.5 angstroms between two different molecules.
  The donor is the molecule of the hydrogen.
- A walk from a random donor follows a random donated bond at each step.
  It never steps straight back to the molecule it came from.
  It stops at the first molecule it has visited.
  The molecules from that visit on are the cycle, three or more of them.
- When the walk finds no cycle, the move returns the state unchanged.
  The hop loop then draws another move for that hop.
- The move has no proposal ratio.

* =examples/h2h_seed=

#+begin_src sh
h2h_seed <lj|morse|tip4p> <n> <rho> <budget> <seed> <arm> <stop> <prefix>
#+end_src

- =arm= :: =recommended= or =base=.
           For =tip4p= these are =Config::recommended_tip4p= and =Config::for_tip4p=.
           For atoms they are =Config::recommended= and =Config::for_cluster=.
- =rho= :: the Morse range.
           Lennard-Jones and TIP4P ignore it.
- =stop= :: an energy at which the run ends early, or =-= to spend the whole budget.
- Start :: atoms come from =random_cluster_in_radius=, drawn from =seed=.
           The radius and the separation are those of =Config::for_cluster(n)=.
           Waters come from =random_tip4p_cluster=, drawn from =seed= times the 64-bit golden ratio plus 3.
           The search itself runs on =seed=.

Outputs:

- =<prefix>.trace.csv= :: =calls,energy= for each new lowest energy, when it is at least 1e-7 lower.
- =<prefix>.xyz= :: the lowest point.
                    Atoms are =X=.
                    Waters are O, H and H, with the M site left out.
                    Their sites use the parameters of the global minimisation program (GMIN).
- Summary on standard output:
  - =calls= :: calls into the potential, capped at the budget.
  - =charged= :: calls the ledger charged.
  - =lowest= :: lowest energy over the counted calls.
  - =geometry_energy= :: the energy of the written geometry.
                         For water it is computed again from the O, H and H sites.
  - =best= :: the search's own best minimum.
  - =hops=, =basins= :: hops made and distinct basins seen.
  - =wall_seconds= :: elapsed time.
