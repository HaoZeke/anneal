* Gupta and reduced cluster kernels

These are C kernels, not =rgpot::Potential= classes.
Lengths are the caller's length unit.
The Gupta arrays and the Sutton-Chen metal rows use Angstrom and eV.
The reduced Lennard-Jones and Morse kernels use the caller's reduced units,
where the well depth and the equilibrium distance are 1.
None of them reads a cell.
A pair at zero separation is not differentiable.

** Gupta

=rgpot_gupta= (=CppCore/rgpot/Gupta/gupta.h=) is the second-moment form of
Gupta, Phys.
Rev. B 23, 6265 (1981),
doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.23.6265, with the parameters of Cleri and Rosato, Phys. Rev. B 48, 22 (1993), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.48.22.
There is no cutoff.
Every pair =i < j= is visited once, in that order.

For one ordered species pair the repulsive term added to the energy is
=2 A exp(-p (r/r0 - 1))= and each atom's density gains =xi^2 exp(-2 q (r/r0 - 1))=.
The energy is that repulsion minus the sum of the square roots of the densities.
=A=, =xi=, =p=, =q= and =r0= are row-major tables of =n_species * n_species=
entries.
=slot[i]= selects the species of atom =i=.

Cabrera-Trujillo et al., arXiv:1007.4009, Table 1, reprint the Cleri-Rosato gold
column: =p = 10.229=, =q = 4.036=, =A = 0.2061= eV, =xi = 1.790= eV and
=sqrt(2) r0 = 4.079= Angstrom, so =r0= is 2.884 Angstrom to three decimals.
Reference value: a dimer at =r = r0= has energy =2 A - 2 xi=.
For that gold row the value is -3.1678 eV.
=GuptaTest= checks the same identity to 1e-12.

The gradient is zeroed before any pair is visited.
The first pair with ~r2 <= 0.0~ sets the energy to positive infinity and
returns, so later pairs are not visited and the gradient stays zero.
Atoms 0 and 1 coincident and atom 2 =NaN= therefore give positive infinity and a
zero gradient.
For =n < 2= the pair loop does not run, so the energy is 0 and the gradient is
zero.
If the scratch allocation fails, the energy is positive infinity and the
gradient is zero.
=NaN= is not ~<= 0.0~.
A pair that is reached with a =NaN= coordinate is evaluated, and the energy and
the gradient become =NaN=.
Null pointers, =slot= entries at or above =n_species=, and =r0= entries that are
not positive are not checked.

** Reduced Lennard-Jones and Morse

=rgpot_cluster_lj= and =rgpot_cambridge_morse= (=cambridge_morse.h=) sum every
pair.
=rgpot_lj_gradient= and =rgpot_morse_gradient= (=ReducedPair.hpp=) are the same
physics with a cutoff: a cutoff of 0 or less keeps every pair, and a positive
cutoff keeps pairs with =r= strictly below it.
The gradient is =dE/dx=, not a force.

The 12-6 form is Lennard-Jones, Proc.
R. Soc.
A 106, 463 (1924),
doi:10.1098/rspa.1924.0082. Wales and Doye, J. Phys. Chem. A 101, 5111 (1997), doi:10.1021/jp970984n,
use it in reduced units for the cluster tables.
Reference value: =epsilon = 1=, =sigma = 1= and a dimer at =r = 2^(1/6)= have
energy -1.
=ReducedPairTest= checks that call to 1e-15, and the gradient to 1e-12.
The same dimer at =r = 1= has energy 0 and an =x= gradient of 24 and -24, which
that test checks exactly.
=rgpot_cluster_lj= checks the minimum to 1e-12.

The Morse form is Morse, Phys.
Rev. 34, 57 (1929), doi:10.1103/PhysRev.34.57.
The reduced cluster term is =epsilon exp(rho (1 - r/re)) (exp(...) - 2)=.
Reference value: =epsilon = 1=, =re = 1= and a dimer at =r = re= have energy -1
and a zero gradient.
=ReducedPairTest= checks both exactly, and =rgpot_cambridge_morse= checks the
unit dimer bit for bit.

=rgpot_tip4p_sites= is the rigid four-site water of Jorgensen et al., J. Chem.
Phys. 79, 926 (1983), doi:10.1063/1.445869, with the caller's oxygen
coefficients.
Sites are molecule-major O, H, H, M. Reference value: two oxygens 2 Angstrom
apart, charges off, =c6 = 2552.24= and =c12 = 2.5104e6=, give
=c12/r^12 - c6/r^6 = 573.011875= in the caller's energy unit.
=ReducedPairTest= requires a bit match to that expression.

** C entry points

*** =rgpot_gupta=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
void rgpot_gupta(const double *x, size_t n, const unsigned char *slot,
                 size_t n_species, const double *a, const double *xi,
                 const double *p, const double *q, const double *r0,
                 double *energy, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =x= (3 n coordinates), =n=, =slot= (one species index per atom),
  =n_species=, and the species-pair tables =a=, =xi=, =p=, =q= and =r0=.
- Units :: =x= and =r0= in the caller's length; =a= and =xi= in the caller's
  energy; =p= and =q= dimensionless.
  The gradient is energy per length.
- Outputs :: one energy and 3 n gradient components.
  For =n < 2= the pair loop does not run, so the energy is 0 and the gradient is
  zero.
- Errors :: no status code and no error message.
  A failed scratch allocation (=realloc= returning null) sets =*energy= to
  =+INFINITY=, zeroes the 3 n entries of =gradient= and returns.
  The first pair with ~r2 <= 0.0~ sets =*energy= to =+INFINITY= and returns.
  The gradient is left at the zeros written before that pair, and later pairs
  are not visited.
  Atoms 0 and 1 coincident and atom 2 =NaN= therefore give =+INFINITY= and a
  zero gradient.
  =NaN= is not ~<= 0.0~, so a pair that is reached with a =NaN= coordinate is
  evaluated and the energy and the gradient become =NaN=.
  Null pointers, =slot= entries at or above =n_species=, and =r0= entries that
  are not positive are not checked.

*** =rgpot_cambridge_morse=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
void rgpot_cambridge_morse(const double *x, size_t n, double rho,
                           double *energy, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =x= (3 n coordinates), =n= and =rho=.
  The well depth and the equilibrium distance are 1.
- Units :: =x= in the reduced length; =rho= is the dimensionless range; energy
  in the reduced energy; gradient in energy per length.
- Outputs :: one energy and 3 n gradient components.
  For =n < 2= the pair loop does not run, so the energy is 0 and the gradient is
  zero.
  For ~n >= 2~ a coincident pair (~r2 <= 0.0~) is skipped, and a =NaN=
  coordinate does not satisfy ~r2 <= 0.0~, so =NaN= propagates into the energy
  and the gradient.
- Errors :: no status code and no error message.
  Null pointers are not checked.

*** =rgpot_cluster_lj=

#+attr_rgpot: signature

void rgpot_cluster_lj(const double *x, size_t n, double *energy,

#+end_src

- Inputs :: =x= (3 n coordinates) and =n=.
  =epsilon= and =sigma= are 1, and there is no cutoff.
- Units :: reduced length and reduced energy.

- Outputs :: one energy and 3 n gradient components.

  For ~n >= 2~ a coincident pair (~r2 <= 0.0~) is skipped, and a =NaN=
  coordinate does not satisfy ~r2 <= 0.0~, so =NaN= propagates into the energy
  and the gradient.
- Errors :: no status code and no error message.
  Null pointers are not checked.

*** =rgpot_lj_pair=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_lj_pair(double epsilon, double sigma, double r2, double *energy,
                  double *dvdr_over_r, double *d2vdr2);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =epsilon=, =sigma=, the squared distance =r2=, and three output
  pointers.
- Units :: =epsilon= in the caller's energy, =sigma= and the distance in the
  caller's length.
  =dvdr_over_r= is =(dE/dr) / r=.
  =d2vdr2= is =d2E/dr2=.
- Outputs :: the pair energy and the two derivatives.
  When =!(r2 > 0.0)= or =r2= is not finite, writes zeros to all three outputs
  and returns 0.
- Errors :: returns 1 when =energy=, =dvdr_over_r= or =d2vdr2= is null, and 0
  otherwise, with no error message.

*** =rgpot_lj_curvature_pairs=

#+attr_rgpot: signature

int rgpot_lj_curvature_pairs(double epsilon, double sigma, int32_t npairs,

                             double *dvdr_over_r, double *d2vdr2);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =epsilon=, =sigma=, =npairs= squared distances and three output

- Units :: as =rgpot_lj_pair=, one entry per pair.

  =energy=, =dvdr_over_r= or =d2vdr2= is null, and 0 otherwise, with no error
  message.

*** =rgpot_lj_gradient=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_lj_gradient(double epsilon, double sigma, double cutoff,
                      int32_t natoms, const double *positions,
                      double *energy, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =epsilon=, =sigma=, =cutoff=, =natoms=, 3 =natoms= coordinates, and
  the energy and gradient pointers.
- Units :: =epsilon= in energy, =sigma= and =cutoff= and positions in length,

  A cutoff of 0 or less keeps every pair.
- Outputs :: one energy and 3 =natoms= gradient components.

- Errors :: returns 1 when =natoms < 0=, =energy= is null, or =natoms > 0= and
  =positions= or =gradient= is null, and 0 otherwise, with no error message.

*** =rgpot_lj_atom_energies=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_lj_atom_energies(double epsilon, double sigma, int32_t natoms,
                           const double *positions, double *atom_energy);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =epsilon=, =sigma=, =natoms=, positions and one energy per atom.
- Units :: as =rgpot_lj_gradient=, with no cutoff.
- Outputs :: each pair's energy added to both atoms.
  The total energy is half the sum of =atom_energy=.
  A pair with =!(r2 > 0.0)= is skipped.
- Errors :: returns 1 when =natoms < 0=, =atom_energy= is null, or =natoms > 0=
  and =positions= is null, and 0 otherwise, with no error message.

*** =rgpot_lj_partial_gradient=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_lj_partial_gradient(double epsilon, double sigma, int32_t natoms,
                              const double *positions, int32_t n_moved,
                              const int32_t *moved, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =epsilon=, =sigma=, =natoms=, positions, =n_moved= atom indices and
  a gradient of 3 =natoms= components.
- Units :: as =rgpot_lj_gradient=, with no cutoff.
- Outputs :: gradient rows of the atoms listed in =moved=.
  Every other row is zero, and pairs with =!(r2 > 0.0)= are skipped.
- Errors :: returns 1 when =gradient= is null, =natoms < 0=,
  =n_moved < 0=, =natoms > 0= and =positions= is null, =n_moved > 0= and =moved=
  is null, or =moved[m] < 0= or ~moved[m] >= natoms~, and 0 otherwise, with no
  error message.
  An index outside =[0, natoms)= is checked after =gradient= is zeroed and after
  the earlier =moved= rows have been accumulated, so the gradient is partial on
  that error.
  The unit dimer at =r = 1= with =epsilon = 1=, =sigma = 1=, =natoms = 2= and
  =moved = {0, 5}= returns 1 and leaves =gradient[0] = 24=, with every other
  component still zero.

*** =rgpot_lj_arithmetic_gradient=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_lj_arithmetic_gradient(const double *sigma, double cutoff,
                                 int32_t natoms, const double *positions,
                                 double *energy, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: one =sigma= per atom, =cutoff=, =natoms=, positions, energy and
  gradient.
  The pair diameter is the arithmetic mean.
- Units :: =sigma=, =cutoff= and positions in length.
  The energy is 4 times the sum of =(s^12 - s^6)= with
  =s = (sigma_i + sigma_j) / (2 r)=, and the well depth is 1.
- Outputs :: one energy and the gradient.
  A cutoff of 0 or less keeps every pair, and a pair with =!(r2 > 0.0)= is
  skipped.
- Errors :: returns 1 when =natoms < 0=, =energy= is null, or =natoms > 0= and
  =sigma=, =positions= or =gradient= is null, and 0 otherwise, with no error
  message.

*** =rgpot_tip4p_sites_config=

- Inputs :: =c6=, =c12=, =epsilon=, =sigma=,
  =use_epsilon= (nonzero selects =epsilon= and =sigma=; zero selects =c6= and =c12=), =coulomb=, =q_h=
  and =q_m=.
- Units :: the caller chooses the energy unit through =c6=, =c12=, =epsilon= and
  =coulomb=.
  =sigma= and the site coordinates share one length.
  =q_h= and =q_m= are charges in the unit =coulomb= converts.
- Outputs :: the parameter block of =rgpot_tip4p_sites=.
- Errors :: none.
  The struct is not checked until the call.

*** =rgpot_tip4p_sites=

#+attr_rgpot: signature
#+begin_src c
int rgpot_tip4p_sites(const rgpot_tip4p_sites_config *cfg,
                      int32_t n_molecules, const double *sites,
                      double *energy, double *gradient);
#+end_src

- Inputs :: =cfg=, =n_molecules=, and =sites= laid out O, H, H, M per molecule
  (12 coordinates).
  The gradient has the same length.
- Units :: the length of =sites= and the energy unit of =cfg=.
- Outputs :: one energy and the gradient.
  Oxygen-oxygen is the Lennard-Jones term and every other site pair is
  =coulomb * q_i * q_j / r=.
- Errors :: returns 1 when =cfg= or =energy= is null,
  =n_molecules < 0=, or =n_molecules > 0= and =sites= or =gradient= is null, and
  0 otherwise, with no error message.
