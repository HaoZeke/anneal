* GLE Mechanics

See [[file:../tutorials/gle-colored-noise.org][gle-colored-noise]] for a
bounded optimization call from a nonoptimal start and its work accounting.

The =eindir= primitives =optimal_sampling_drift= and =GleThermostat= construct
the fitted drift and its discrete thermostat update. The anneal driver in
=src/methods/gle_langevin.rs= owns the BAB position/force steps and temperature
schedule. Its augmented momentum matrix contains one physical row and =N_s=
auxiliary rows. All rows persist across epochs and scale together when the
temperature changes; independent driver invocations initialize separate state.

The standalone driver and gradient portfolio arm call this implementation.
The communicating box and atomic hop drivers do not call it as an escape
operator. Reusing the thermostat in another driver requires explicit ownership
of its retained state, coordinate-boundary handling and charged callbacks;
the component algebra alone does not supply those connections.
