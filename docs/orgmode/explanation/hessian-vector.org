* Hessian-vector products

The entry points are in the [[file:../reference/c_abi.org][C ABI reference]].

A minimum-mode search (Lanczos, dimer) applies the Hessian =H= of the energy to
many directions =v= at one geometry.
With only a force callback each product is a difference of two force
evaluations, and one of them is the centre the search has not moved from.
A potential handle answers =H v= itself and keeps the forces of that centre.

** Cost and error

The forward scheme reuses the forces of the centre.
=k= forward products at one centre therefore cost =k + 1= force calls instead of
=2k=.
No finite difference makes one call per product at =O(h^2)=.
The forward error is =(h/2) T(u, u) |v|=, with =T= the third derivative of the
energy, and cancelling it takes the second evaluation that the central scheme
makes.
A search that needs second-order products at one call each registers an analytic
product.
The forward product is positively homogeneous in =direction= but not odd: the
=O(h)= term keeps its sign when =v= does not.
