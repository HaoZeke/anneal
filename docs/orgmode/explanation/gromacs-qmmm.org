* GROMACS QM/MM through the CP2K interface

The steps are in
[[file:../howto/engine-gromacs-qmmm.org][GROMACS QM/MM drop-in]], and the input
keys and limits are in
[[file:../reference/gromacs_dropin.org][GROMACS QM/MM drop-in reference]].

GROMACS 2024 to 2026 has three in-process hooks for an external potential.

- QM/MM through libcp2k (=-DGMX_CP2K=ON=) calls a fixed C API, in double
  precision, every step, and passes the topology elements through a PDB file.
  Its energy is a GROMACS energy term.
  It carries no virial: =QMMMForceProvider= adds its forces without a virial
  contribution.
- NNPot (2025 and later) loads only TorchScript =.pt= models, converts positions
  to single precision, and also carries no virial.
- PLUMED applies a bias.
  It carries the virial but keeps the energy out of the GROMACS energy terms.

MiMiC serves CPMD only, and GROMACS has no i-PI client.
The libcp2k API is the one that takes any potential without a model format and
reports the energy as GROMACS' own, so the drop-in implements it.
