=GaussJacobiQuad= =select_method_auto=).

| Feature | Source |
|---------+--------|
| =dim= | objective bounds |
| =has_grad= | caller supplied gradient |

* Values-only runs under Auto

With =policy="auto"=, no =grad_fn= and no =noise_sigma=, the driver does
not consult the regime: every such run, whatever its box width or
dimension, goes to one values-only loop over six arms.

| Arm | Starts from |
|-----+-------------|
| =cma= | BIPOP CMA-ES centred on the incumbent: local runs with short steps, large-population runs with a box-wide step; separable above 100 dimensions or when the budget is shorter than the covariance learning horizon |
| =qn= | finite-difference projected BFGS from the incumbent, kicked from it once converged |
| =gsa= | one chain at the incumbent (dual_annealing's =x0=), quenched: each temperature step visits every coordinate once at the cooling visit scale, box-wide early and local late, and keeps only improvements; no local search |
| =de= | best/1/bin with the incumbent as the first population member (differential_evolution's =x0=) |
| =surrogate= | an additive model fitted to every evaluation so far: one evaluation tests its per-coordinate minimum, then tempered samples from its marginals |
| =explore= | QMC restarts over the whole box |

Without =x0= the start is the best of a seeded low-discrepancy design of
=dim + 1= points (at most a tenth of the budget), so seeds vary it; with
=x0= it is =x0=. A slice is four gradients' worth of evaluations or a
48th of the budget (eight rounds for each of the six arms), whichever is
larger, but at most the geometric mean of the budget and four gradients'
worth: past 48 times 48 four-gradient slices the number of slices keeps
growing, as the square root of the budget, instead of stopping at 48.
When the budget affords five finite-difference gradients per
coordinate, a descent from the start opens the run and continues while
its slices lower the incumbent, up to convergence; its successes
describe the start, so =qn= then rejoins the ranking on the prior.

With less, a descent from the start converges only by taking most of
the run, which would leave =gsa= and =de= no turn, and separable
multimodal objectives (shifted Rastrigin, Styblinski-Tang, Levy) need
theirs. Such a run opens instead with one slice each of =cma= and the
descent from the start, then two slices of =gsa= from the incumbent,
whose quenched visits leave the start's basin one coordinate at a time,
and then a descent turn that settles the basin they reach and, as in
the rounds, keeps the turn while it pays.

From there =gsa= and then =cma= each keep the turn while they pay. A
phase ends once it has gone eight slices without lowering the
incumbent, or as long as its last gain took if that is longer. After an
opening it stops short of the closing descent plus one slice for each
other arm not yet played, the next phase's included; without one, short
of the closing descent plus one slice for =de= until it has played, since
slices held there for the surrogate and the restart arm cost the phases
more than those arms return. Against a descended incumbent a GSA record
is a lower basin and a CMA-ES gain a better point of the same one; both
come in bursts (records as the anneal cools, a run per covariance) that
single slices would not see.

Each arm not yet played then takes a turn (the warm-up round, as in
the main bandit), after which a round picks uniformly with probability
=1/round=, otherwise replays the arm whose last turn lowered the
incumbent, and otherwise takes a discounted Thompson draw. A turn is one
slice, except that =qn= keeps the turn while each slice lowers its
descent's value by more than the success threshold, until that descent
converges and short of the closing descent: a kicked descent climbs
before it can beat the incumbent, and one from a poor start
(Lennard-Jones positions with overlapping atoms) takes many slices to
settle, so the turn is scored on the basin the descent reaches. A turn
succeeds when it lowers the incumbent by more than =1e-4 max(|f|, 1)=.
The descent closes the run with the rest of the budget, which holds
ten gradients' worth of evaluations back for it (at most 40% of the
budget), enough for a descent to converge. It goes on with its current
descent, which may be a kick from the incumbent, and restarts at the
incumbent only if another arm has lowered it since =qn= last played.

* Regimes → preferred portfolio arms / kernels

These rows govern runs with a gradient or a declared noise scale; Legacy
always uses =Default=.

| Regime | When | Prefer |
|--------+------+--------|
| =StochasticNoise= | noise_sigma set | OSA accept required; explore + GSA; refuse exact-only accept path |
| =MultimodalGlobal= | mean box width > 9 and dim ≥ 2 | GSA and DE first, after a short multimodality probe on boxes narrower than 50 |
| =LowDimSmooth= | dim ≤ 5 and gradient | shift/hop/GLE polish-heavy; early QMC polish |
| =HighDimIllConditioned= | dim ≥ 20 and gradient | GLE, reduced-space, hop; surrogates deprioritized |
| =MultimodalNoGrad= | no gradient, dim ≥ 5 or aspect ≥ 8 | reported by =select_regime= but never run by the driver (values-only Auto runs take the loop above) |
| =Default= | else | balanced library order (explore first for restart floor) |

The rows are tested in this order, so a wide box with a gradient is
=MultimodalGlobal= even when dim ≤ 5.

* Safety

- In the main bandit the restart arm (=explore=) always remains first so
  Borel–Cantelli restart still fires. In the values-only loop the floor
  the guarantee needs is asymptotic. The opening runs while it improves
  by the success threshold, which cannot last on a bounded objective,
  and after that every arm keeps a uniform share and is pulled infinitely
  often as the budget grows. The phases and the descent's turns stop for
  the same reason: each success lowers the incumbent, and each slice that
  extends a turn lowers its descent, by at least =1e-4=, so a bounded
  objective allows a number of them that does not grow with the budget,
  while the number of slices does. The =1/round= picks, whose sum
  diverges, then reach every arm. At a given budget nothing is reserved
  for the floor. Short of the opening budget =gsa= plays right after the
  descent's first slice whenever a slice is left beyond the closing
  descent. The warm-up round gives =de=, =surrogate= and =explore= (and,
  after an opening, =gsa=) a slice each when the budget left after the
  opening or the first turns, the phases and the descent's turns holds
  those slices and the closing descent. The phases leave room for all of
  them after an opening and for =de= without one; a descent's turn does
  not. On Lennard-Jones positions at 39 dimensions and 1000 evaluations
  =gsa= takes the two turns after the descent's first slice, the closing
  descent the rest, and =de=, =surrogate= and =explore= do not play.
- Unknown features fall to =Default= (safe fallback).
- Policy biases Beta priors and arm *order*, not hard exclusion (except
  noise-aware accept requirement under =StochasticNoise=).
