* D2: GLE move-kernel stationarity at fixed temperature
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d2-gle-move-kernel-stationarity-at-fixed-temperature
:END:
** Setup
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: setup
:END:
The generalized Langevin equation (GLE) thermostat in
=eindir/src/gle.rs= realizes colored-noise dynamics through a Markovian
augmentation. The physical coordinate \(x \in \mathbb{R}^d\) carries
momentum \(p\), and \(n_s\) auxiliary momenta
\(s = (s_1, \dots, s_{n_s})\) shape a frequency-dependent friction.
Stack the augmented momentum state as
\(z = (p, s_1, \dots, s_{n_s})^\top \in
\mathbb{R}^{n_s + 1}\) (per physical coordinate; coordinates decouple in
the bath so we analyze one). The continuous dynamics is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot{x} &= p, \\
dz &= -A_p\, z\, dt \;-\; (\nabla f(x))\, e_0\, dt \;+\; B_p\, dW,
\end{aligned}
\tag{D2.1}
\]

where \(e_0 = (1, 0, \dots, 0)^\top\) injects the force only into the
physical momentum, \(A_p \in \mathbb{R}^{(n_s+1)\times(n_s+1)}\) is the
drift acting on \((p, s)\), \(B_p\) the diffusion, and \(W\) a standard
Wiener process. The drift and diffusion satisfy the
*fluctuation-dissipation* relation (the convention in
=eindir/src/gle.rs=, lines 191-192)

\[
A_p\, C \;+\; C\, A_p^\top \;=\; B_p\, B_p^\top,
\qquad C = T\, I,
\tag{D2.2}
\]

the *sampling* (canonical) thermostat: the augmented covariance is
isotropic at temperature \(T\). The "a la carte" property is that (D2.2)
fixes correctness (through \(C\)) while leaving \(A_p\) free to tune
acceleration.

For the fitted =optimal_sampling_drift=, each bath block is a damped
oscillator with eigenvalues \(-\Gamma_k \pm i\Omega_k\), skew-coupled to
\(p\). The oscillator and coupling terms are skew, so \(A_p + A_p^\top\)
is diagonal and non-negative; with \(C = T I\), (D2.2) gives
\(B_p B_p^\top = T (A_p + A_p^\top)\), a diagonal positive-semidefinite
matrix, hence a valid noise covariance.

** Part (a): the augmented OU sub-dynamics leaves \(N(0, C)\) invariant exactly
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: part-a-the-augmented-ou-sub-dynamics-leaves-n0-c-invariant-exactly
:END:
Freeze \(x\) (drop the force term), so the bath obeys the linear OU SDE

\[
dz \;=\; -A_p\, z\, dt + B_p\, dW.
\tag{D2.3}
\]

*** Proposition D2.1 (continuous OU invariance)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: proposition-d2.1-continuous-ou-invariance
:END:
If (D2.2) holds with \(C = T I\), then \(N(0, C)\) is the unique
stationary distribution of (D2.3), provided \(A_p\) is positive-stable
(all eigenvalues have positive real part) and \((A_p, B_p)\) is
controllable.

*Proof.* The covariance \(\Sigma(t) = \mathrm{Cov}(z_t)\) of a linear OU
process solves the matrix differential equation
\(\dot{\Sigma} = -A_p \Sigma - \Sigma
A_p^\top + B_p B_p^\top\). A stationary covariance \(\Sigma_\infty\) is
a fixed point, i.e. solves the Lyapunov equation
\(A_p \Sigma_\infty + \Sigma_\infty
A_p^\top = B_p B_p^\top\). By (D2.2), \(\Sigma_\infty = C = T I\) is
exactly such a solution. Positive-stability of \(A_p\) makes the
Lyapunov solution unique, and the OU process is Gaussian, so its
stationary law is \(N(0, C)\). The Gaussian density
\(\rho_C(z) \propto \exp(-z^\top C^{-1} z / 2) = \exp(-\|z\|^2 / 2T)\)
is the tempered Maxwell-Boltzmann momentum law, as required.
\(\qquad\blacksquare\)

*** Proposition D2.2 (exact discrete OU step preserves \(C\) identically)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: proposition-d2.2-exact-discrete-ou-step-preserves-c-identically
:END:
The integrator does not discretize (D2.3) approximately; it uses the
*exact* OU transition. For step \(dt\), set

\[
\mathrm{T} = e^{-dt\, A_p},
\qquad
S S^\top = C - \mathrm{T}\, C\, \mathrm{T}^\top,
\qquad
z' = \mathrm{T}\, z + S\, \xi,\quad \xi \sim N(0, I).
\tag{D2.4}
\]

If \(z \sim N(0, C)\) then \(z' \sim N(0, C)\) exactly, with no
\(O(dt)\) or \(O(dt^2)\) error.

*Proof.* \(z'\) is Gaussian with mean zero and covariance

\[
\mathrm{Cov}(z') = \mathrm{T}\, C\, \mathrm{T}^\top + S S^\top
= \mathrm{T}\, C\, \mathrm{T}^\top + \big(C - \mathrm{T}\, C\, \mathrm{T}^\top\big)
= C.
\]

The cancellation is algebraic and holds for any \(\mathrm{T}\), so the
choice (D2.4) makes \(N(0, C)\) stationary for the discrete bath step to
machine precision; the only error is the floating-point evaluation of
\(e^{-dt A_p}\) and the LDL\(^\top\) root of \(S S^\top\) (=ldl_sqrt=).
The positive-semidefiniteness of \(C - \mathrm{T} C \mathrm{T}^\top\)
(needed for a real \(S\)) follows from (D2.2):
\(C - \mathrm{T} C \mathrm{T}^\top = \int_0^{dt} e^{-\tau A_p} B_p B_p^\top
e^{-\tau A_p^\top}\, d\tau \succeq 0\). \(\qquad\blacksquare\)

This is why =eindir/src/gle.rs= documents that the bath leaves the
Boltzmann momentum law invariant for *any* valid drift \(A_p\):
correctness rides on (D2.2) and (D2.4) alone, independent of the
acceleration tuning.

** Part (b): BAB splitting, harmonic-limit invariant, and anharmonic bias
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: part-b-bab-splitting-harmonic-limit-invariant-and-anharmonic-bias
:END:
The full Move (=anneal/src/methods/gle_langevin.rs=) integrates (D2.1)
by a symmetric BAB splitting. Write the generator
\(\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_B +
\mathcal{L}_A\), where the deterministic Hamiltonian flow (\(B\) blocks:
position drift and force kick) is

\[
\mathcal{L}_A = p \cdot \nabla_x - \nabla f(x)\cdot \nabla_p,
\]

and the OU bath (\(B\) thermostat half-steps) is
\(\mathcal{L}_B = -\,(A_p z)\cdot
\nabla_z + \tfrac{1}{2}(B_p B_p^\top) : \nabla_z \nabla_z\). The BAB
step over \(dt\) composes

\[
\Phi_{dt}^{\mathrm{BAB}}
= e^{\frac{dt}{2}\mathcal{L}_B}\;
  e^{dt\,\mathcal{L}_A}\;
  e^{\frac{dt}{2}\mathcal{L}_B},
\tag{D2.5}
\]

with the *exact* OU sub-flow \(e^{\frac{dt}{2}\mathcal{L}_B}\) from
(D2.4).

*** Harmonic limit
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: harmonic-limit
:END:
Suppose \(f\) is quadratic, \(f(x) = \tfrac12 x^\top H x\) with
\(H \succ 0\). Then \(\mathcal{L}_A\) generates a /linear/ Hamiltonian
flow and the joint \((x, z)\)-dynamics is a linear OU process. The
target tempered Gibbs measure on \((x, p)\) is

\[
\pi_T(x, p) \;\propto\; \exp\!\Big(-\tfrac{1}{T}\big[f(x) + \tfrac12 p^\top p\big]\Big),
\tag{D2.6}
\]

a Gaussian with covariance \(\mathrm{diag}(T H^{-1}, T I)\) on
\((x, p)\), augmented by \(N(0, T I)\) on the auxiliary \(s\). Each
factor of (D2.5) preserves the appropriate Gaussian exactly: the OU
half-steps preserve the momentum/auxiliary Gaussian by Proposition D2.2,
and the symplectic linear flow \(e^{dt
\mathcal{L}_A}\) preserves the joint quadratic Hamiltonian's Gibbs
Gaussian exactly (Verlet/leapfrog is exact-in-distribution for Gaussian
targets under a linear force, because it is a symplectic linear map
conjugate to the exact flow). The composition of measure-preserving maps
preserves the product Gaussian, so:

#+begin_quote
In the harmonic limit the BAB integrator with exact OU sub-step has
invariant distribution equal to the tempered Gibbs measure (D2.6) on the
\((x, p)\) marginals, with *zero discretization bias*, for every \(dt\)
in the leapfrog stability region \(dt < 2/\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(H)}\).

#+end_quote

*** Anharmonic bias
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: anharmonic-bias
:END:
For general \(C^3\) anharmonic \(f\), the splitting (D2.5) no longer
preserves \(\pi_T\) exactly. The Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff expansion of
(D2.5) gives a modified generator
\(\widetilde{\mathcal{L}} = \mathcal{L} + dt^2\, \mathcal{E} +
O(dt^4)\) (the symmetric BAB split kills the \(O(dt)\) term, so the
leading error is \(O(dt^2)\)). The stationary distribution
\(\widetilde{\pi}_{T, dt}\) of the discrete chain differs from \(\pi_T\)
by

\[
\widetilde{\pi}_{T, dt}(x, p)
= \pi_T(x, p)\,\big(1 + dt^2\, h(x, p) + O(dt^4)\big),
\tag{D2.7}
\]

with \(h\) a bounded function determined by \(\mathcal{E}\) and
depending on the third and fourth derivatives of \(f\). The bias in any
smooth observable \(g\) is

\[
\Big|\,\mathbb{E}_{\widetilde{\pi}_{T, dt}}[g] - \mathbb{E}_{\pi_T}[g]\,\Big|
\;=\; O(dt^2),
\tag{D2.8}
\]

the standard weak order-2 accuracy of symmetric splitting integrators
for Langevin dynamics (Leimkuhler and Matthews, 2015, Chapter 7;
Bou-Rabee and Owhadi, 2010). The constant scales with
\(\sup\|\nabla^3 f\|\) and \(\sup\|\nabla^4 f\|\) on the support. *To
remove the \(O(dt^2)\) bias entirely*, wrap the GLE proposal in a
Metropolis correction against the true \(f\) (the GLE Move feeds the
\(\mathrm{Accept}\) slot exactly as in D1); this is the route the
algebra makes available without changing the bath.

** Part (c): ergodicity of the continuous dynamics (Hormander conditions)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: part-c-ergodicity-of-the-continuous-dynamics-hormander-conditions
:END:
For the continuous dynamics (D2.1) to sample
\(\pi_T(x) \propto e^{-f(x)/T}\) *marginally* in \(x\), two conditions
suffice.

*(C1) Hypoellipticity (Hormander's bracket condition).* Noise enters
only the auxiliary/physical momentum channel (\(B_p\) has rank
\(\le n_s + 1\) and zero rows on the \(x\) block), so the generator
\(\mathcal{L}\) is degenerate-elliptic. The dynamics is hypoelliptic
when the Lie algebra generated by the noise vector fields
\(\{B_p e_i\}\) and their iterated commutators with the drift
\(\{A_p, \nabla f\}\) spans the full tangent space
\(\mathbb{R}^{d(n_s+2)}\) at every point. Concretely, the position \(x\)
receives randomness only through the chain
\(\text{noise} \to s \to p \to x\); the bracket
\([\mathcal{L}_A, \mathcal{L}_B]\) must propagate fluctuations from the
bath into \(p\) and then into \(x\). For the damped-oscillator drift
with non-zero coupling \(c_k \ne 0\) and a non-degenerate force Hessian,
the bracket condition holds (Hormander, 1967; Pavliotis, 2014, Section
6.3). This gives a smooth transition density.

*(C2) Confinement / drift condition.* \(f\) is \(C^2\), bounded below,
and grows at infinity (\(f(x) \to \infty\) as \(\|x\| \to \infty\), or
\(f\) is confined to the bounded box with reflecting/clamped boundaries)
so that a Lyapunov function
\(V(x, z) = f(x) + \tfrac12 \|z\|^2 + \text{const}\) yields a geometric
drift condition \(\mathcal{L} V \le -\alpha V + \beta\, \mathbf{1}_K\)
on a compact set \(K\).

Under (C1)+(C2), the augmented process is geometrically ergodic with
unique invariant measure
\(\propto \exp\!\big(-\tfrac1T[f(x) + \tfrac12\|z\|^2]\big)\)
(Mattingly, Stuart and Higham, 2002; Pavliotis, 2014, Theorem 6.16).
Marginally integrating out \(z\) recovers the Gibbs target

\[
\int \exp\!\Big(-\tfrac1T\big[f(x) + \tfrac12 \|z\|^2\big]\Big)\, dz
\;\propto\; e^{-f(x)/T} \;=\; Z_f(T)\, \pi_T(x),
\]

so the \(x\)-marginal of the stationary law is exactly \(\pi_T\). The
colored-noise spectrum set by \(A_p\) controls the /rate/ of convergence
(the optimal-sampling fit flattens the worst normalized efficiency to
\(0.13\) versus \(0.07\) for the best single white-noise friction,
=eindir/src/gle.rs= line 169) but not the /target/, which (D2.2) pins to
\(\pi_T\).

** Connection to the algebra
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: connection-to-the-algebra
:END:
The GLE bath sits entirely in the \(\mathrm{Move}\) slot: it is a
temperature-indexed proposal kernel \(\mathrm{Move}(x, T)\) whose
invariant momentum law is \(N(0, T I)\) by Part (a). Law (L4)
(temperature monotonicity of acceptance) is untouched because the bath
proposes; acceptance stays in its own slot. When the GLE proposal is
Metropolized against the true \(f\), laws (L1)/(L3) of the
\(\mathrm{Accept}\) slot remove the \(O(dt^2)\) anharmonic bias of Part
(b), giving exact sampling of \(\pi_T\) at fixed \(T\) regardless of
step size.

** Witness
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: witness
:END:
=proofs/d2_gle_stationarity.py= verifies symbolically on a \(2\times2\)
block (\(n_s = 1\)): the FDT relation
\(A_p C + C A_p^\top = T(A_p + A_p^\top)\) is symmetric and diagonal;
\(C = T I\) solves the Lyapunov equation; and the exact discrete OU step
\(z' = \mathrm{T} z + S\xi\) with \(SS^\top = C - \mathrm{T} C
\mathrm{T}^\top\) preserves \(\mathrm{Cov} = C\) for *any* propagator
\(\mathrm{T}\) (so the invariance is an identity, not an approximation).
Numerically it confirms FDT and covariance preservation for the fitted
\(n_s = 12\) =optimal_sampling_drift=. Tests in
=proofs/tests/test_d2_gle_stationarity.py=.
