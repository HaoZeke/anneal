* Part I — Screening

**Safe screen (S1).** Fraction \(\rho\) of quenches are identifiable as doomed
(return-to-same minimum, unphysical) with zero false-negative rate on the GM
event. Force ratio versus no screen:
\[
\eta(\rho)=1-\rho\frac{Q}{q_0+Q}<1\quad(\rho,Q>0).
\]
\(\partial\eta/\partial\rho<0\), \(\partial\eta/\partial Q\le 0\).

**Lossy random screen (S2).** Thin all proposals by \(\rho\) and kill a
fraction \(\delta\) of true successes. Relative expected force to first success:
\[
\frac{E}{E_0}=\frac{1-\rho Q/(q_0+Q)}{(1-\rho)(1-\delta)}.
\]
**Theorem.** For all \(\rho\in[0,1)\), \(\delta\ge 0\), \(q_0,Q\ge 0\),
\[
\frac{E}{E_0}\ge 1
\]
when \(\delta=0\) (identity \(E/E_0-1=\rho q_0/((1-\rho)(q_0+Q))\ge 0\)).
Random thinning never reduces expected force; only *safe* identification of
doomed proposals (S1) helps.

* Part II — Costly bias

D12 free \(\gamma_*=b/b_{\max}\). With cost \(C(\gamma)\) paid from budget \(B\),
need
\[
\frac{b}{\gamma}<b_{\max}\bigl(g,d,B-C(\gamma)\bigr).
\]
Linear model \(C(\gamma)=c_0(\gamma-1)_+\). Define
\[
\phi(\gamma)=\gamma\ln\bigl(B-c_0(\gamma-1)+e\bigr),\qquad
\kappa=\frac{bd}{2g}.
\]
Feasible iff \(\max\phi>\kappa\). \(\phi\) has an interior maximum; large \(c_0\)
can make the maximum fail on LJ75-scale \((\kappa\approx 808,\gamma_*\approx 54)\).
Then **no MetaD-style paid bias reopens the D6∩D7 window** — multi-start / MH /
CSA remain necessary.

Free bias \(c_0=0\) recovers D12: \(\gamma>\gamma_*\).

* Design consequences

1. Implement **return / same-basin screens** (S1); do not randomly skip quenches.
2. Budget accounting for bias: if building \(\gamma\sim 50\) costs too much force,
   bias cannot substitute for catchment multi-start (D13).
3. Combine: S1 saves force *inside* each seed; D13 spends force *across* seeds.
