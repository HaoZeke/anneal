#+TITLE: D13: Two-funnel force scaling and multi-start optimality
#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:t

Executable: =proofs/d13_two_funnel_force.py=. Parents: D12 (force ledger),
landscape facts (Wales: once lowest icosahedral, Marks never).

* Model

After setup cost \(Q_0\), a seed enters good funnel \(G\) with probability
\(\alpha\) and trap funnel \(I\) with probability \(1-\alpha\).

- In \(G\): each hop succeeds (hits GM) with probability \(p\).
- In \(I\): inter-funnel escape probability per hop is \(\varepsilon\ge 0\).
  The hard Wales regime is \(\varepsilon=0\).

Each post-setup hop costs \(Q\) force units.

* Proposition 1 (seed success, \(\varepsilon=0\))

\[
P_{\mathrm{seed}}(\alpha,p,H)=\alpha\bigl(1-(1-p)^{H}\bigr).
\]

* Proposition 2 (multi-start and sequential force)

With \(n\) independent seeds,
\[
P=1-\bigl(1-P_{\mathrm{seed}}\bigr)^{n}.
\]
If \(H\to\infty\) and \(0<p\le 1\), then \(P_{\mathrm{seed}}\to\alpha\) and
\[
\mathbb{E}[\text{seeds to first success}]=\frac1\alpha,\qquad
\mathbb{E}[\text{force}]=F_s/\alpha
\]
for sequential seeds each costing \(F_s\).

* Proposition 3 (catchment lower bound)

Under \(\varepsilon=0\), even if hops inside \(G\) are free and always succeed,
expected force to eventual success is at least \(Q_0/\alpha\). Local refinement
cannot remove the \(1/\alpha\) factor. Multi-start (or any mechanism that
improves effective \(\alpha\)) is mandatory.

* Proposition 5 (global split \(n\) vs \(H\))

Under total force \(F=n(Q_0+HQ)\),
\[
P(n,H)=1\Bigl[1-\alpha\bigl(1-(1-p)^{H}\bigr)\Bigr]^{n}.
\]
Grid maximizer =optimal_split=. Special cases:

- \(p=1\): prefer many seeds with \(H\ge 1\) (α-limited).
- \(\alpha=1\): prefer large \(H\), small \(n\) (p-limited).
- interior \((\alpha,p)\): tradeoff; restarts beat one long chain when
  \(\varepsilon=0\) and \(\alpha\) is not near 1.

* Proposition 6 (escape)

\(P_{\mathrm{seed}}\) is nondecreasing in \(\varepsilon\); \(\varepsilon>0\)
strictly beats \(\varepsilon=0\) at fixed \((\alpha,p,H)\) (mixture over escape
time + local Geo(\(p\))).

* Design consequences for anneal / JCC

1. BFWT empty window (D12) + \(\varepsilon=0\) ⇒ **restarts / population / CSA**,
   not longer single Metropolis chains.
2. Report **catchment diagnostics** (funnel identity) early; kill seeds in \(I\).
3. Force scales as \(1/\alpha\): improving basin keying that raises effective
   \(\alpha\) is first-order; polishing \(p\) inside \(I\) is worthless.
