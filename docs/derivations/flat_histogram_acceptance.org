#+TITLE: Accepting by entropy: the diagnosis holds, the cure is refuted
#+OPTIONS: toc:2

* The claim under test

Basin hopping samples minima from \(\pi(m) \propto e^{-\tilde E_m/T}\). Marginalised
onto energy that is \(p(\tilde E) \propto g(\tilde E)\,e^{-\tilde E/T}\), with \(g\) the
density of minima. On a multi-funnel landscape \(g\) is exponentially larger in
the wide funnel than in the one holding the global minimum, so at a temperature
loose enough to keep the chain moving the multiplicity decides where it sits,
and at a temperature tight enough for energy to win the chain stops moving.

The proposed cure weights states by \(1/g\) instead. The marginal in energy
becomes flat, the deep and rare energies take the same share of the run as the
shallow and abundant ones, and with \(S = \ln g\) the acceptance is Metropolis
with entropy in place of energy,

\[ a = \min\left(1,\ e^{-[S(\tilde E_{\text{new}}) - S(\tilde E_{\text{old}})]}\right). \]

* The mechanism is real in isolation

On a landscape built to contain the obstruction and nothing else -- two funnels
joined by a single high-energy bridge, the narrow one walked down a rung at a
time, and leaving the bridge landing in a funnel in proportion to how many
states it holds -- the rule solves 8 of 8 where Metropolis solves 0 of 8 at
every temperature in a seven-point sweep from 0.1 to 2.0.

That number is the weakest evidence here, not the strongest. The landscape was
written by the same hand testing the hypothesis, and it reached total separation
only on the third attempt: the first version let a uniform proposal reach the
target directly, the second omitted the entropic pull-back, and an intermediate
version passed for the wrong reason entirely (see below). A landscape tuned
until it separates cannot falsify anything. It shows the mechanism /can/ work.

* Three defects found by that test, each of which alone inverts it

** The estimator was Wang-Landau in disguise

Refitting against the newest sweep and adding the residual to a running mean is
the Wang-Landau recursion, and it carries the Wang-Landau defect
(Belardinelli and Pereyra, doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.75.046701): 400 samples over 96
bins is four per bin, so each update injects about half a nat and the curve
random-walks by more than the structure it estimates. \(S\) is now fitted against
every recorded sweep at once, each with the curve it ran under, by a multinomial
likelihood that profiles out the per-sweep constant.

Correcting this turned the toy from 8/8 to 3/8. *The first pass had been won by
the estimator's noise, not by the mechanism.* A result banked as confirmation
was an artifact, and only the correction found it.

** Extrapolating multiplicity upward charges for barrier crossing

The second-difference prior carried the observed trend up through never-visited
bins, asserting that unobserved high energies hold ever more states. That
charges the chain for exactly the rise it must make to leave a funnel, which
rebuilds the trap the target exists to dissolve. Above the highest bin ever
visited the curve is held flat: no evidence there supports anything else.
Downward extrapolation stays, being both the useful direction and the safe one
to be wrong in. This took the toy from 3/8 to 7/8, and capping at the highest
/visited/ bin rather than at the window edge took it to 8/8.

** The density of states was binned on the biased energy

Deposits accumulate during a run, so the histogram sat on a coordinate drifting
underneath it. It is over bare quenched energy now, with the bias additive in
the exponent as under Metropolis.

* On the real landscape it is refuted

38 points, 400000 charged evaluations, 24 seeds per arm, identical moves, seeds
and budget across arms.

| arm                              | solved | note                       |
|----------------------------------+--------+----------------------------|
| Metropolis + basin bias          |  15/24 | the control                |
| flat, no cut                     |   3/24 |                            |
| flat, cut at q95 / q50 / q10     | 4/3/3  | corrected estimator        |
| flat, cut at q50 / q25 / q10     | 3/1/5  | pre-correction             |
| Metropolis, bias off             |   6/24 |                            |
| flat, bias off                   |   0/24 |                            |

Posterior comparison, Beta-Binomial under a Jeffreys prior:

| comparison               | P(treat > ctrl) | difference          |    BF |
|--------------------------+-----------------+---------------------+-------|
| flat vs control, bias on |          0.0004 | -0.440 [-0.663, -0.189] | 56.89 |
| flat vs control, bias off |          0.0020 | -0.240 [-0.430, -0.078] | 15.89 |

Five configurations across three campaigns, every one between 0/24 and 5/24.
The consistency is itself the evidence.

* Why, and it is not about entropy

At equal charged budget the bias-off arms ran *12409 hops (flat) against 5700
(Metropolis)*. The flat arm bought more than twice as many hops with the same
evaluations, and solved nothing.

Flat sampling delivers exactly what it promises: equal residence at every
energy. The unstated premise is that a unit of residence costs the same
everywhere, and on this problem it does not. A high-energy trial is killed by
the screen after a partial descent; a low-energy one has to be relaxed to
convergence. Cost per state falls with energy by more than a factor of two, so
*flat in energy is, under a fixed evaluation budget, a policy that prefers cheap
shallow states* -- and it prefers them in proportion to how useless they are.

The multicanonical construction of Berg and Neuhaus
(doi:10.1016/0370-2693(91)90256-P) was built to compute thermodynamics across an
energy range, where equal residence is the goal and every sample costs the same.
Neither premise holds for a search under a budget.

* What the control says

Turning off the basin bias takes Metropolis from 15/24 to 6/24
(P = 0.996, difference +0.360 [+0.097, +0.597], BF 7.65). The deposits are
carrying a large part of the search, and depositing into visited basins is
itself an entropy-defeating device: it fills the funnel the chain is trapped in.

So the diagnosis survives and the cure does not. The landscape is entropically
trapped, this driver already answers that with a mechanism measured at BF 7.65,
and replacing the acceptance rule removes something that works in favour of
something that spends the budget on the cheapest states available.

* Status

Refuted as a search rule at 38 points. The module stands, its six tests pass,
and the estimator is correct in a way the sweep-by-sweep recursion is not; what
is refuted is the claim that flat-in-energy acceptance finds minima under a
fixed evaluation budget. No stopping rule and no default follows from it, and
the arm is off unless asked for.
