#+TITLE: The quenched-energy sample does not carry a usable floor
#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:t

* Status

Branch =feat/tail-endpoint=, commits =969e663= through =4a4a1b3=, not merged
and not pushed. =src/tail.rs= carries the construction and its sixteen tests,
=examples/tail_classifier.rs= the retrospective measurement,
=Outcome::quenched= and =Config::trace_quenched= the recorder. This note draws
them into one place. The finding is negative and the negative is the result.

* Abstract

A basin-hopping run pays for one local minimisation per hop and reads a single
number off each. It keeps the running minimum and discards the rest, which is a
sample from the density of states of minima in whatever region the chain
occupies. That sample was put to a question the running minimum cannot answer,
namely whether the occupied region contains a minimum as deep as the target,
and the question needs no collective variable, no template and no knowledge of
the target morphology.

Peaks over threshold on the negated energies gives a generalised Pareto law
whose shape decides the matter: negative shape means a finite floor at
=u + sigma/(-xi)=, non-negative means no floor the sample can see. A posterior
over =(sigma, xi)= by grid quadrature turns that floor into a decision with an
interval on it.

Measured at 38 points over 96 seeds and 400000 charged evaluations, the
decision carries no information. The probability that a run which failed
carries a higher exclusion probability than one which succeeded is 0.43 to 0.56
with 95 per cent bootstrap intervals spanning 0.5 at every prefix of the
budget, and the Bayes factor for unequal exclusion rates against one shared
rate is 0.19 to 0.47, favouring equality by four to five to one. The reason is
not that the shape comes out non-negative. It is that the generalised Pareto
approximation has not taken hold anywhere in the range 900 quenched minima
resolve: the endpoint drifts monotonically deeper as the threshold rises and
never stabilises, and the moment endpoint the same data imply sits above an
energy the run had already reached in 543 of 576 threshold fits.

* 1 The construction

** 1.1 Model

Write the quenched energies as =e_1, ..., e_m=, negate them, =y_i = -e_i=, so
the deepest minimum is the largest =y=. Let =F= be the distribution of =Y= and
=y_F = sup{y : F(y) < 1}= its upper endpoint. Pickands' theorem, and
independently Balkema and de Haan's, says =F= lies in the maximum domain of
attraction of an extreme value law if and only if the conditional excess
distribution

: F_u(y) = P(Y - u <= y | Y > u)

converges uniformly, as =u -> y_F=, to a generalised Pareto law

: G(y; sigma, xi) = 1 - (1 + xi y / sigma)^(-1/xi),   xi != 0
: G(y; sigma, 0)  = 1 - exp(-y / sigma)

supported on =y >= 0= for =xi >= 0= and on =0 <= y <= sigma/(-xi)= for =xi < 0=.
Pickands, /Statistical inference using extreme order statistics/, Ann. Statist.
3 (1975) 119-131, [[https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176343003][doi:10.1214/aos/1176343003]]; Balkema and de Haan, /Residual
life time at great age/, Ann. Probab. 2 (1974) 792-804,
[[https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176996548][doi:10.1214/aop/1176996548]]. Both DOIs resolve.

With =xi < 0= the fitted law has upper endpoint =theta_Y = u + sigma/(-xi)=,
which on the energy scale is the lower endpoint

: theta_E = e_u - sigma/(-xi),      e_u = -u,

the deepest minimum the sampled region contains.

** 1.2 Why a posterior

=theta_E= is a ratio of the two parameters and diverges as =xi -> 0-=, so its
sampling distribution is skewed however large the sample is and a plug-in
=sigma_hat / (-xi_hat)= inherits none of that skew. Smith, /Maximum likelihood
estimation in a class of nonregular cases/, Biometrika 72 (1985) 67-90,
[[https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/72.1.67][doi:10.1093/biomet/72.1.67]], shows the likelihood theory itself fails: the
maximum likelihood estimator is asymptotically normal only for =xi > -1/2=, has
a non-normal limit on =-1 < xi <= -1/2=, and does not exist for =xi <= -1=,
which is exactly the range where the floor is sharpest. The likelihood remains
a likelihood throughout; it is the point summary that stops meaning anything.

** 1.3 Prior

In =(log sigma, xi)= coordinates,

: pi(log sigma, xi) propto exp(-xi^2 / (2 tau^2)) . 1[-1 < xi < 2],   tau = 0.5.

Two separate choices. The scale carries =pi(sigma) propto 1/sigma=, flat in
=log sigma=, the right-Haar prior for a scale parameter and the only one under
which the endpoint posterior is equivariant: reporting in units of the pair
well depth or in kJ/mol then gives the same answer rescaled rather than two
different answers. The shape carries a normal centred at zero, because zero is
the boundary between a region with a floor and a region without one, so
centring there commits to neither; =tau = 0.5= puts 95 per cent of the mass on
=|xi| < 0.98=, covering the range shape estimates are reported in. The lower
truncation at =-1= is where the likelihood stops being bounded and discards
prior mass 2.275e-2; the upper truncation discards 3.17e-5 and exists only to
bound the quadrature box. The prior is proper, so nothing rests on the
propriety arguments needed for improper reference priors (Northrop and
Attalides, Statist. Sinica 26 (2016) 721-743, [[https://doi.org/10.5705/ss.2014.034][doi:10.5705/ss.2014.034]]).

=TailPosterior::p_xi_floor= reports posterior mass against the lower
truncation, so a fit the truncation is driving is visible as such. Over the
measured runs it is below 1e-13 wherever the shape is identified, and the one
configuration where it was not, the first recorder, is in Section 3.

** 1.4 Quadrature

The posterior is computed on a grid rather than sampled, so there is no chain
to diagnose. A coarse 97 by 121 pass locates the box holding everything within
30 log units of the peak; a refined 161 by 161 pass over that box carries the
weights. Equal spacing in =(log sigma, xi)= makes the scale prior a constant,
so the rectangle rule reduces to weights proportional to the exponentiated log
posterior. Two containment numbers are reported rather than assumed:
=box_leak=, the coarse mass outside the refined box, measured below 1.3e-13 on
the campaign fits, and a grid-doubling test asserting the reported quantiles
move less than 5e-3 between 81 and 321 nodes per axis.

* 2 Tests against closed forms

Sixteen tests, ten against closed forms.

| Identity                                          | Test                                    |
|---------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------|
| density integrates to the cdf on a bounded window | =gpd_is_a_distribution=, 2e-5           |
| quantile inverts the cdf                          | same, 1e-10                             |
| endpoint =sigma/(-xi)=, mean =sigma/(1-xi)=       | =gpd_closed_forms=, 1e-12               |
| =sigma_1 = sigma_0 + xi (u_1 - u_0)=              | =threshold_invariance_of_the_endpoint=  |
| endpoint invariant under threshold raise          | same, 1e-12                             |
| conditional excess law equals the raised law      | same, 1e-12                             |
| =xi = 0= posterior is InvGamma(k, S)              | =exponential_slice_is_inverse_gamma=    |
| =xi = -1= posterior is Pareto(y_max, k)           | =uniform_slice_gives_a_pareto_endpoint= |
| mean excess slope is =xi / (1 - xi)=              | =mean_excess_slope_returns_the_shape=   |
| moment endpoint recovers the true one             | same, 6 per cent                        |

The inverse-gamma test pins the quadrature mean to 1e-6 relative and the
variance to 1e-4 relative against =S/(k-1)= and =S^2/((k-1)^2 (k-2))=. The
Pareto test pins the endpoint quantiles to 1e-4 relative against
=y_max (1-q)^(-1/k)=, at four quantiles.

Three further tests exercise the whole construction rather than a piece.
=endpoint_intervals_cover= draws 120 synthetic samples of 400 from
GPD(1, -0.3) and requires the nominal 90 per cent credible interval to cover
the true endpoint between 0.80 and 0.98, three binomial standard errors either
side. =refuses_an_endpoint_when_there_is_none= is the false-positive control:
2000 exponential draws must return =P(xi >= 0) > 0.2= and a shape mean inside
0.1 of zero, so the machinery cannot manufacture a floor.
=endpoint_never_sits_above_the_deepest_sample= walks every quadrature node and
requires the support constraint to hold at each.

* 3 What the recorder had to be

The first recorder kept 342 rows from a run of 12000 hops and its fits piled
against the shape truncation at =xi= near =-0.97=. Two separate defects, both
worth stating because both are invisible in a summary log.

The screen truncates 97 per cent of trials at a 25-step partial descent, and a
partial energy lands wherever the descent stopped, so it is a draw from the
stopping rule rather than from the density of states. No threshold excludes
such a value. Gating the recorder on the full-relaxation branch leaves 363
minima per run at 38 points, and =SCREEN_MARGIN= above the energy range turns
the screen off for a comparator that pays 254 charged evaluations per hop
against 33 and returns 1587.

The second defect is ties. A chain in a funnel re-quenches to the same
structure repeatedly: 1589 relaxations return 934 distinct energies and the
deepest value of a run arrives six to nineteen times. A tie at the sample
maximum is what a generalised Pareto fit reads as a hard cutoff, and it drove
the shape to the prior floor with the endpoint pinned at the value already
found. Deduplicating to distinct minima, two agreeing to 1e-6 being the same
minimum where the deepest four at 38 points are separated by 0.68 and more, is
the correct sample: the question is a property of the set of minima the region
contains, not of how often the chain visits each.

The gate itself is a selection on energy, since the screen relaxes only trials
whose partial energy sits within the margin of the incumbent. That removes deep
minima reached from poor starts and pushes a fitted endpoint upward, which is
the direction that would make the classifier look better. Both arms were
therefore measured and both are reported.

* 4 Threshold stability

Under the model the endpoint does not depend on the threshold, by the identity
of Section 2. So the endpoint posterior computed along a ladder must agree
wherever the approximation has taken hold and disagree below it, and that is
the diagnostic, in place of a percentile picked by convention. The rule
=select_threshold= takes the lowest rung whose endpoint posterior overlaps
every higher rung by at least 0.5, the top rung being the least biased and most
diffuse; the whole ladder is reported so the rule can be second-guessed.

One failed run at 400000 charged evaluations, deepest minimum =-173.2524=:

second failed run drifts from =-173.36= to =-180.43=, a third from =-173.60= to

with no stable region to choose from.

* 5 The model refuted by the data it was fitted to

The mean excess is the model-free discriminant and needs no prior, no
quadrature and no fit. For generalised Pareto excesses with =xi < 1=,

: E[Y - u | Y > u] = (sigma + xi (u - u_0)) / (1 - xi),

linear in the threshold with slope =xi / (1 - xi)=. Least squares over the
ladder gives an implied shape of median =-0.4197= over the 62 failed runs, 5th
to 95th percentile =[-0.5426, -0.3294]=, and =-0.4676= over the 34 solved,
=[-0.5923, -0.4016]=. Clearly negative, and clearly at odds with the fitted
=E[xi]= of the table above, which runs from =-0.31= at the deepest threshold to
=+0.08= at the shallowest tail.

Inverting the same first moment for the scale gives the moment endpoint

: theta_E = u - m (1 - xi) / (-xi),

a floor the model asserts, carrying no support constraint because no likelihood
was involved. In 543 of 576 threshold fits that floor sits above an energy the
run had already reached, the worst by 1.9984 in energy units against a
funnel-to-funnel gap of 0.68. The model is refuted at the thresholds where it
has the most data.

The likelihood fit hides this because its support constraint forbids an
endpoint above an observed minimum. The 97.5 per cent quantile of the endpoint
posterior sits a median 0.3470 below the deepest sampled energy, 5th to 95th
percentile =[0.0821, 2.9902]=. The posterior is pinned by the constraint rather
than located by the tail's shape, which is why the decision statistic tracks
the running minimum: the thing it was meant to improve on.

* 6 The classifier

The falsifiable claim was: for runs that fail, does the endpoint posterior
exclude the reference =-173.928427=, and for runs that succeed, does it include
it.

The prefix is what makes this a prediction rather than arithmetic. A run that
succeeds has the reference in its own sample, and the support constraint then
forces the endpoint at or below it, so a call on the whole trace of a
successful run is a tautology. Successful runs contribute only at prefixes
ending before their first encounter, which is why the solved column thins from
30 to 0 as the prefix grows.

** 6.1 Discrimination, and the control it has to beat

A single decision bar hides the shape of the statistic, so the whole ordering
is reported: the probability that a run which failed carries a higher exclusion
probability than one which succeeded, over all pairs, ties at a half, chance
0.5. The interval is a bootstrap over runs rather than a Beta posterior on a
concordance count, because the pairs are not independent and a Beta interval
would be several times too narrow.

The control is the same quantity for the deepest energy found so far. The
endpoint posterior is anchored just below that value by the support constraint
and would look informative merely by restating it, so a construction that does
not beat this control has bought nothing.

0.62, 0.35, 0.27, 0.19, 0.12. The two curves lie on top of each other. No bar
separates them.

** 6.3 The production configuration

With the screen on, 363 relaxations per run leave too few distinct minima for
the ladder: only 15 whole-trace fits and 9 usable mean-excess slopes survive
the sample-size gate across 96 seeds. What survives agrees. Implied shape
median =-0.3086= failed against =-0.2284= solved; the moment endpoint sits
above an energy already reached in 22 of 47 threshold fits; the discrimination
runs 0.25 to 0.68 with every bootstrap interval spanning 0.5. The unscreened
arm is the evidence and this is the consistency check.

* 7 Verdict

The claim is false as stated and the reason is not the one offered as the
likely failure.

The shape does not come out non-negative. Three independent routes put it near
=-0.4=: the mean-excess slope over the whole trace, =-0.42= for failed runs and
=-0.47= for solved; the fitted posterior at the lowest threshold, =-0.31= to
=-0.43=; and the direction of the drift. The energies do behave, over the bulk,
like a distribution with a floor.

The generalised Pareto approximation has not taken hold at these sample sizes.
The endpoint drifts monotonically deeper by 2.4 energy units across the
threshold ladder and dissolves into unbounded at the top rung; no lower rung
overlaps the top by more than 0.44; and the moment endpoint the same data imply
sits above a minimum the run already found in 94 per cent of threshold fits.
Nine hundred distinct minima per run at 38 points is not enough to reach the
regime the limit theorem describes, and the campaign's largest samples are of
that order.

What the fitted posterior reports instead is the support constraint. Its 97.5
per cent quantile sits a median 0.347 below the deepest sampled energy, so the
decision statistic is a re-expression of the running minimum, and the running
minimum at half budget does not separate the two funnels either: 0.475 by the
same ranking measure, interval =[0.340, 0.615]=.

The construction is worth keeping. It is tested against ten closed forms, it
reports its own containment error, its shape-truncation mass and its
threshold-stability diagnostic, and every one of those diagnostics fired
correctly on data where the model does not hold. That is what a diagnostic is
for. The two defects it exposed in the recorder, partial-descent energies and
funnel-revisit ties, are both properties of how a basin-hopping run reads its
own landscape, and both would have silently produced a confident wrong answer.

What it does not license is a stopping rule. Nothing here should be built into
the search.

* 8 Placement

=src/tail.rs=, alongside =src/terminate.rs=, which is the other module that
decides when to stop. The recorder belongs where it is, on
=cluster_hopping::Config= and =Outcome=, because the trace has to be a prefix
of the run to be a prediction, and only the driver can make it one.
