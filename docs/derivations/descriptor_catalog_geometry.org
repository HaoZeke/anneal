* Scope

This note records the mathematical contracts implemented by
=DescriptorSpace=, =regularized_pullback=, =BasinCatalog=, and =BasinCensus=.
The descriptor has no reference energy, named target structure, or runtime
morphology label.  System signatures determine which transformations and
records are admissible.

* D20: quotient descriptor pseudometric

For one system signature =s=, let =X_s= contain the valid Cartesian
configurations and let =G_s= contain exactly the signature-preserving
translations, proper rotations, and like-species permutations.  The physical
configuration space is

\[
  {\cal Q}_s = X_s/G_s.
\]

The versioned descriptor map is =p_s : X_s -> R^m=.  Every block is an
invariant aggregate of local SOAP or ACE features and is normalized
independently.  The catalog distance is

\[
  d_s([x],[y]) = ||p_s(x)-p_s(y)||_2.
\]

The local SOAP power spectrum and ACE contractions are unchanged by proper
rotations.  Mean and diagonal central-moment aggregation are unchanged by atom
reordering.  Species channels restrict that reordering to like species.
Therefore =p_s(gx)=p_s(x)= for every =g in G_s=, and =d_s= is well-defined on
the quotient.

Nonnegativity and symmetry follow from the Euclidean norm.  The Euclidean
triangle inequality gives =d_s(a,c) <= d_s(a,b)+d_s(b,c)=.  Distinct quotient
points can have the same finite descriptor, so identity of indiscernibles is
not asserted.  The result is a pseudometric.  Exact minimum identity remains
separate validation evidence.

The implementation rejects distance evaluation unless the schema name,
version, block ordering, cutoffs, dimensions, and normalization layout agree.
=tests/descriptor_invariance.rs= checks allowed transformations, schema
isolation, the pseudometric laws, and separation of named LJ75 diagnostic
fixtures without consulting their labels during descriptor evaluation.

* Normalized multiscale blocks

Let =q_i in R^r= denote a local invariant vector for atom =i= and let =N= be
the atom count.  The mean and diagonal second central moment are

\[
  \mu = {1\over N}\sum_i q_i,
  \qquad
  v = {1\over N}\sum_i (q_i-\mu)\odot(q_i-\mu).
\]

An ACE nu=3 block uses the corresponding mean of the local contracted
features.  For every raw block =b != 0=, the stored block is
=\widehat b=b/||b||_2=.  Zero raw blocks remain zero.  Block metadata records
the aggregation kind, radial and angular resolutions, cutoff, vector interval,
raw norm, and normalization convention.

* Analytic descriptor Jacobian

Let =L_i = partial q_i / partial x= be the analytic local SOAP or ACE
Jacobian.  The raw mean and diagonal-variance derivatives are

\[
  {\partial \mu\over\partial x}={1\over N}\sum_i L_i,
  \qquad
  {\partial v\over\partial x}={2\over N}\sum_i
  \mathop{diag}(q_i-\mu)L_i.
\]

The second identity uses =sum_i(q_i-mu)=0=.  For a nonzero raw block =b= with
Jacobian =B=, differentiation of its L2 normalization gives

\[
  {\partial \widehat b\over\partial x}
  = {B\over ||b||_2}-{b(b^T B)\over ||b||_2^3}.
\]

=DescriptorSpace::jacobian_analytic= applies these equations block by block.
=DescriptorSpace::jacobian_fd= supplies an independent central-difference
check.  =tests/descriptor_pullback.rs= compares the full normalized,
species-aware multiscale Jacobian with central differences.

* D21: constrained regularized pullback

For desired descriptor increment =Delta p=, positive diagonal weight =W=,
damping =lambda > 0=, and allowed Cartesian subspace projector =P=, define
=A=J P=.  The Cartesian increment minimizes

\[
  ||A z-\Delta p||_W^2 + \lambda^2||z||_2^2,
  \qquad \Delta x=Pz.
\]

The first-order condition is

\[
  (A^T W A+\lambda^2 I)z=A^T W\Delta p.
\]

Positive damping makes the normal matrix positive definite, so the
implementation uses Cholesky factorization.  The projector removes frozen
coordinate axes, global translation when allowed by the signature, and
first-order pair stretches inside rigid groups.

For =W^(1/2) A = U Sigma V^T=, the realized descriptor component along a left
singular vector is multiplied by

\[
  {\sigma_i^2\over\sigma_i^2+\lambda^2} \in [0,1).
\]

Thus the linearized map cannot amplify an attainable requested component.
This does not imply global contraction of the nonlinear descriptor.  The
implementation bounds =||Delta x||_2/L_0= by a trust radius and measures the
descriptor after applying the step; noncontraction is a classified proposal
rejection at the policy boundary.

=proofs/tests/test_descriptor_pullback.py= checks the normal equations and
singular attenuation.  =tests/descriptor_pullback.rs= checks analytic and
finite-difference Jacobians, rank deficiency, frozen coordinates, rigid modes,
trust clipping, and measured nonlinear contraction.

* D22: active-catalog packing and incumbent invariants

Let =C_t= be the finite active catalog and let =D_t= be its coordinator-owned
packing threshold.  Admission maintains

\[
  i \ne j \Longrightarrow d_s(c_i,c_j) \ge D_t.
\]

The threshold schedule is nonincreasing.  A candidate conflicting with active
entries replaces the complete conflict set only when its energy is lower than
every conflicting representative.  Removing that set eliminates all candidate
conflicts, while distances among retained entries are unchanged.  A separated
candidate is added when capacity permits.  At capacity it can replace the
worst nonincumbent only when it has lower energy.  These cases preserve
capacity and pairwise packing.

Same-neighborhood updates retain only the lower-energy representative.  The
incumbent is the least-energy validated representative and therefore has
monotone nonincreasing energy.  Every active record retains its census basin
identifier and provenance.

The initial-population scale is

\[
  \overline D={2\over M(M-1)}\sum_{i<j}d_s(p_i,p_j),
  \qquad D_0=\min(\overline D,D_{\min}).
\]

Using =D_0 <= D_min= makes the initial set packing-safe.  The schedule decreases
geometrically over charged ensemble work to
=min(D_0,max(D_census,0.4D_0))=.  =tests/basin_catalog.rs= checks these cases
and their invariants under generated admission streams.

* D23: fixed-census Good--Turing saturation

The census assigns every validated charged quench to exactly one immutable
descriptor neighborhood.  Let =n_j= be the visit count for census basin =j=,
=n=sum_j n_j=, and =N_1=sum_j 1[n_j=1]=.  The unseen-mass estimator is

\[
  \widehat P_0={N_1\over n}.
\]

It is undefined for =n=0=.  The policy declares saturation only when =n >= 20=
and =N_1/n < 0.20=.  Active-catalog replacement does not change census
medoids, membership, or counts, so eviction cannot erase visits or change the
statistical species being counted.  =tests/basin_census.rs= checks exact count
conservation, immutable medoids, deterministic assignment, and saturation from
census visits only.
