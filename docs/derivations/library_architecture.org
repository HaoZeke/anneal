#+TITLE: ELPA-shaped restructuring of the anneal primitives

The measured campaigns are the profile; this is the kernel work they demand.
System-independent throughout: every item multiplies throughput for any
objective, any move stack, any driver.

* Two-stage quench (full -> banded -> tridiagonal analogue)

The quench becomes a pipeline of stages with contracts, replacing the
screen-steps constant: stage 1 the existing partial screen, stage 2 a
constrained settle of the moved atoms only (RATTLE-style: unmoved bond
network as holonomic constraints), stage 3 full polish for survivors.
Honest cost: a k-atom settle is k/n pair terms per evaluation, charged
fractionally with an audited cost model. Every productive move is one to
three atoms; stage 2 is where the order of magnitude lives.

* Blocked proposals over shared structure

Every kernel recomputes O(n^2) structure per call: SurfaceRelocate its
coordination counts, the graph key its distances, ring profiles their
adjacency. One incremental NeighborTable per incumbent, updated in O(k)
after a k-atom move and shared by kernels, keys, rings and settle, is the
blocked-kernel analogue: compute shared structure once, use it across the
batch. This is the single largest constant-factor item in the library.

* Composable driver stages

cluster_hopping is a monolith of flags. The primitives it hides -- propose,
screen, settle, polish, identify, deposit, allocate, exchange -- become
traits with contracts, so basin hopping, tempering and any population
driver compose the same audited kernels instead of reimplementing them.
The eindir component algebra already does this for accept/cool/move at the
SA layer; the cluster layer gets the same treatment.

* Spectral embedding of the archive by rational filtering

The SOAP vectors of the archive are long and sparse and the edge graph over
the minima network needs only a spectral window of its Laplacian, not a full
diagonalisation. That is the FEAST shape (Polizzi,
doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.79.115112): a rational filter selects the eigenspace in
a chosen interval from sparse matrix-vector products alone, and refreshing
the subspace as the archive grows reuses the previous subspace as the
starting basis. The embedding then supplies coordinates for aggressive
inter-cluster proposals along the network's principal directions.

* Status

Spec committed as the working plan; NeighborTable and the staged quench are
the implementation order, with the fractional-charge audit before any
measurement that uses it.
