* D5: Work-unit-optimal terminal polish reserve
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d5-endgame-switch
:END:

** Scope
A portfolio holds remaining budget \(B\) work units, spent on exploration
slices of size \(b\) (each improves the incumbent basin with stationary
probability \(\theta\)) and polish steps of size \(w\) that contract the
relative gap geometrically: \(g_k = g_0\rho^k\) with \(0<\rho<1\).
The cell is won if the final relative gap is at most \(\tau\) (CUTEst
near-best protocol). This derivation fixes the *terminal polish reserve*
and the explore-first schedule form used by =global_optimize=.

** Algorithm D5 (polish reserve)
1. Estimate contraction \(\hat\rho\) from work-normalized log-ratios of
   consecutive polish improvements (exact on geometric sequences).
2. Compute \(n^{*}=\max\{0,\lceil\ln(\tau/g)/\ln\hat\rho\rceil\}\) polish
   steps needed to convert the incumbent basin (or the quantile form used
   by the NIG posterior in the implementation).
3. Reserve \(p^{*}=\mathrm{clamp}(n^{*}w,\,B/4,\,B/3)\) work units (asymmetric
   floor: never below a quarter of total budget).
4. Run all remaining exploration *before* any reserved polish (exchange
   optimality). Spend the reserve as uninterrupted polish on \(x_{\mathrm{best}}\).

** Identities (checked by =proofs/d5_endgame_switch.py=)
- Continuous switch \(n_c=\log(\tau/g)/\log\rho\) satisfies \(g\rho^{n_c}=\tau\).
- \(n^{*}=\lceil n_c\rceil\) is minimal with \(g\rho^{n^{*}}\le\tau\).
- Log-ratio estimator recovers \(\rho\) exactly on geometric gaps.
- Among schedules with \(e\) explorations and \(p\) polish steps, explore-first
  maximizes win probability (exact enumeration).
- Under explore-first, win probability is maximized at \(p=n^{*}\).
