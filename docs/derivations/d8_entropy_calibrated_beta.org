* D8: Entropy-calibrated inverse temperature for residual population control
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-entropy-calibrated-inverse-temperature
:END:

** Scope and quantity being optimized
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-scope
:END:
A multi-walker residual population-control step holds a fixed target size
\(N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\) and must turn raw energies \(E_1,\ldots,E_N\) into
offspring masses \(m_i\ge 0\) with \(\sum_i m_i = N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\).
The classical DMC weight \(m_i \propto e^{-\beta E_i}\) introduces a free
scale \(\beta\). Engineering schedules pick \(\beta\) from a gap heuristic;
this derivation *calibrates* \(\beta\) by matching the Shannon entropy of
the normalized weight law to a budget-progress target. The optimized
quantity is the free-energy functional whose unique minimizer is the
exponential family at the calibrated \(\beta^\star\).

** Assumptions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-assumptions
:END:
1. Finite energies \(E_i\in\mathbb{R}\) for \(i=1,\ldots,N\) with \(N\ge 2\).
2. Residual (or multinomial) resampling uses normalized masses
   \(p_i(\beta)=e^{-\beta E_i}/Z(\beta)\), \(Z(\beta)=\sum_j e^{-\beta E_j}\).
3. Progress parameter \(\rho\in[0,1]\) is known (evaluation budget spent /
   total budget). No Monte Carlo noise enters the identities below.

** Free-energy model and optimal masses
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-free-energy
:END:
For a fixed \(\beta>0\), the entropic free-energy problem on the simplex is

\begin{equation}
\min_{p\in\Delta^{N-1}}
  \underbrace{\sum_i p_i E_i}_{\text{mean energy}}
  +\frac{1}{\beta}\underbrace{\sum_i p_i\log p_i}_{\text{negative entropy}}.
\tag{D8.1}
\end{equation}

*** Proposition D8.1 (softmax is the unique minimizer)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-prop-softmax
:END:
The unique minimizer of (D8.1) is
\[
p_i^\star(\beta)
  =\frac{e^{-\beta E_i}}{\sum_j e^{-\beta E_j}}.
\tag{D8.2}
\]

/Proof./ Lagrangian \(\mathcal{L}=\sum p_i E_i+(1/\beta)\sum p_i\log p_i
+\lambda(1-\sum p_i)\). Stationarity:
\[
E_i+\frac{1}{\beta}(\log p_i+1)-\lambda=0
\implies p_i\propto e^{-\beta E_i}.
\]
Strict convexity of \(p\mapsto\sum p\log p\) on the simplex yields uniqueness. \(\square\)

Expected offspring under target size are \(m_i=N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\,p_i^\star(\beta)\).
Residual resampling takes \(\lfloor m_i\rfloor\) copies and multinomial-fills
the fractional remainders (Liu, /Monte Carlo Strategies in Scientific Computing/).

** Entropy as a monotone thermometer
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-entropy-thermometer
:END:
Define the Shannon entropy of the exponential family
\begin{equation}
H(\beta)
  :=-\sum_i p_i(\beta)\log p_i(\beta)
  =\log Z(\beta)+\beta\,\langle E\rangle_\beta,
\tag{D8.3}
\end{equation}
where \(\langle E\rangle_\beta=\sum_i p_i(\beta)E_i\).

*** Proposition D8.2 (derivative identity)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-prop-derivative
:END:
For all \(\beta\ge 0\),
\begin{equation}
H'(\beta)=-\beta\,\mathrm{Var}_{p(\beta)}(E).
\tag{D8.4}
\end{equation}
In particular \(H\) is nonincreasing; if not all \(E_i\) are equal then
\(H'(\beta)<0\) for every \(\beta>0\).

/Proof./ Standard exponential-family calculus:
\[
\frac{\partial\log Z}{\partial\beta}=-\langle E\rangle,\qquad
\frac{\partial\langle E\rangle}{\partial\beta}=-\mathrm{Var}(E).
\]
Differentiate (D8.3):
\begin{align*}
H'
  &=\frac{Z'}{Z}+\langle E\rangle+\beta\langle E\rangle'
  =-\langle E\rangle+\langle E\rangle+\beta\bigl(-\mathrm{Var}(E)\bigr)
  =-\beta\,\mathrm{Var}(E).
\end{align*}
\(\square\)

*** Proposition D8.3 (endpoint limits)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-prop-limits
:END:
\[
H(0)=\log N,\qquad
\lim_{\beta\to\infty}H(\beta)=\log m,
\]
where \(m=\#\{i:E_i=\min_j E_j\}\) is the multiplicity of the lowest energy.

/Proof./ At \(\beta=0\), \(p_i=1/N\) so \(H=\log N\). As \(\beta\to\infty\),
mass concentrates on the argmin set of size \(m\), yielding entropy \(\log m\). \(\square\)

*** Corollary D8.4 (unique entropy calibration)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-cor-unique
:END:
If not all energies coincide, then for every target
\(H_\star\in(\log m,\log N]\) there exists a unique \(\beta^\star\ge 0\)
with \(H(\beta^\star)=H_\star\). Continuity of \(H\), strict decrease on
\((0,\infty)\), and the endpoint limits give the claim. \(\square\)

** Progress-linked target entropy
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-target-schedule
:END:
Let \(\rho\in[0,1]\) be the fraction of the evaluation budget spent and
\(f\in(0,1]\) an elite-floor factor (default \(f=1/N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\) so
\(\log(fN)\) tends to \(\log 1=0\) only in the extreme; we use
\(f=\max(2,N)/N\) so \(H_{\mathrm{floor}}=\log 2\) unless \(N=1\)).
Set the target entropy by linear interpolation in log-population space:
\begin{equation}
H_\star(\rho)
  =(1-\rho)\log N
  +\rho\log\bigl(\max\{1,\,f\,N\}\bigr).
\tag{D8.5}
\end{equation}
Early search (\(\rho\approx 0\)) keeps \(H_\star\approx\log N\) (near-uniform
branching, exploration). Late search (\(\rho\to 1\)) forces low entropy
(concentration on elites).

A safe bisection upper bound uses the range \(R=\max_i E_i-\min_i E_i\):
\begin{equation}
\beta_{\max}
  =\begin{cases}
     0 & R\le 0,\\
     \dfrac{20\log N}{R} & R>0,
   \end{cases}
\tag{D8.6}
\end{equation}
which drives \(H(\beta_{\max})\) near \(\log m\) for nondegenerate spectra.

** Algorithm D8 (ECIT residual population control)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-algorithm
:END:
Input: energies \(\{E_i\}_{i=1}^N\), target size \(N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\),
progress \(\rho\in[0,1]\), elite-floor factor \(f\in(0,1]\), RNG.

1. Shift energies by \(E_i\leftarrow E_i-\min_j E_j\) (numerically stable softmax).
2. Compute \(H_\star\) from (D8.5) with the *current* population size \(N\).
3. If all finite \(E_i\) are equal, set \(\beta^\star=0\); else bisect on
   \([0,\beta_{\max}]\) (D8.6) until \(H(\beta^\star)=H_\star\) within
   absolute tolerance \(10^{-8}\) (or max 64 iterations).
4. Form \(p_i=p_i^\star(\beta^\star)\) by (D8.2); set expected counts
   \(m_i=N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\,p_i\).
5. Residual resampling: place \(\lfloor m_i\rfloor\) copies of walker \(i\);
   multinomial-draw the remaining \(N_{\mathrm{tgt}}-\sum\lfloor m_i\rfloor\)
   slots from residual masses \(m_i-\lfloor m_i\rfloor\).
6. Return the controlled population of size \(N_{\mathrm{tgt}}\).

Output: offspring walkers and the calibrated \(\beta^\star\).

** Relation to D6–D7 and to engineering \(\beta\) schedules
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: d8-relation
:END:
D6 constrains *local Metropolis temperature* for one-step descent on a
sphere; D7 constrains escape windows under a budget. D8 calibrates the
*population-control* inverse temperature so residual branching realizes a
prescribed diversity (entropy) rather than an ad-hoc gap scale
\(\beta\sim c/\mathrm{gap}\). The free-energy problem (D8.1) is the
variational origin of the exponential weights already used by DMC-style
arms; the new operating law is the entropy target (D8.5) and the unique
inverse map \(\beta^\star=H^{-1}(H_\star)\).
