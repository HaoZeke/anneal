* Proposition A (window emptiness)

Let \(g=f-f_{\mathrm{best}}>0\), dimension \(d\ge 1\), remaining work \(B\ge 0\),
barrier proxy \(b\ge 0\), and \(e=\mathrm{e}\). Define
\[
T_{\mathrm{hi}}=\frac{2g}{d},\qquad
T_{\mathrm{lo}}=\frac{b}{\ln(B+e)},\qquad
b_{\max}(g,d,B)=\frac{2g\ln(B+e)}{d}.
\]
Then
\[
T_{\mathrm{lo}}<T_{\mathrm{hi}}
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
b\,d < 2g\ln(B+e)
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
b < b_{\max}(g,d,B).
\]
When the inequality fails the D6∩D7 window is empty: no single Metropolis
temperature simultaneously satisfies the D6 descent ceiling and the D7
escape floor.

*Proof.* Cross-multiply positive quantities \(d\) and \(\ln(B+e)\). \(\square\)

**Corollary (emptiness ratio).** \(r=b/b_{\max}\). The window is empty iff
\(r>1\).

**LJ75 plateau plug-in (literature \(b\), campaign \(g\)).**
With \(b=8.69\), \(g=1.21\), \(d=225\), \(B=3\cdot 10^{6}\),
\[
b_{\max}\approx 0.160,\qquad r\approx 54.2,\qquad
T_{\mathrm{lo}}\approx 0.583,\qquad T_{\mathrm{hi}}\approx 0.0108.
\]
The window is empty by more than an order of magnitude. Increasing \(B\) to
\(10^{7}\) changes only the logarithm. Even \(g=50\) still yields
\(b_{\max}\approx 6.63<8.69\).

* Proposition B (force ledger on independent hops)

Suppose each hop (proposal + local minimization to a new quenched minimum)
charges \(Q\ge 0\) force evaluations in expectation, and successive hops
succeed independently with probability \(p\in(0,1]\) (success = landing the
target global minimum under the protocol). Let \(N\sim\mathrm{Geo}(p)\) on
\(\{1,2,\ldots\}\). Then
\[
\mathbb{E}[N]=\frac{1}{p},\qquad
\mathbb{E}[\text{force to first success}]=\frac{Q}{p}.
\]

*Proof.* \(\mathbb{E}[N]=\sum_{k\ge 1}k(1-p)^{k-1}p=1/p\); linearity. \(\square\)

**Fixed multi-seed budget.** \(n\) seeds each run \(H\) hops:
\[
\text{force}=n\,H\,Q.
\]
If each seed has success probability \(p\) independently,
\[
\mathbb{P}(\text{at least one success})=1-(1-p)^{n}.
\]

These identities convert hop-success rates (Wales–Doye style) into force
units under a charged ledger without new physical assumptions.

* Proposition C (bias reopening)

If a history-dependent bias reduces the *effective* barrier to \(b/\gamma\)
with \(\gamma\ge 1\) (well-tempered MetaD depth factor in the D7 commentary),
then the window is nonempty iff
\[
\frac{b}{\gamma}<b_{\max}(g,d,B)
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
\gamma>\gamma_{*}:=\frac{b}{b_{\max}(g,d,B)}=\frac{b\,d}{2g\ln(B+e)}.
\]
On the LJ75 plateau numbers, \(\gamma_{*}\approx 54\). A collective-variable
bias that does not reduce effective inter-funnel depth cannot reopen the
window, regardless of schedule.

* Effective dimension (link to D6 anisotropy)

For \(f=x^{\top}Hx/2\) and an *isotropic* proposal, the small-step critical
temperature is \(T_c=x^{\top}H^{2}x/\mathrm{tr}H\), which collapses along
soft modes by a factor of order \(d/\kappa\). A proposal covariance
proportional to \(H^{-1}\) whitens the quadratic and restores
\(T_c=2f/d\) (\(\theta_c=2\)). Ambient coordinate dimension \(3N\) is not
the right \(d\) for D6 once the chain lives in a soft subspace; covariance
adaptation is the mechanism that restores the sphere ceiling. This does
**not** reopen a multi-funnel barrier of height \(b\gg b_{\max}\); it only
repairs local descent inside a basin.
