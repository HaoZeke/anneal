#+TITLE: GPMD: Gap-Proportional Metropolis Descent
#+SUBTITLE: Derivation by Pólya’s four phases
#+OPTIONS: toc:2

* (I) Understand the problem

** What is the unknown?
A temperature law \(T=T(x)\) and proposal scale rule for a Metropolis chain
so that, on a *local* quadratic basin, the expected one-step decrease of the
*current state energy* is positive and near-optimal under a standard
evolution-strategy proposal scaling.

** What are the data?
- Smooth objective \(f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}\) (locally \(f(x)=\|x\|^2/2\)
  from the basin bottom).
- Isotropic Gaussian proposal \(y=x+\sigma z\), \(z\sim N(0,I_d)\), with
  ES scaling \(\sigma=c\|x\|/d\) (Rechenberg).
- Metropolis acceptance \(\min\{1,e^{-\Delta/T}\}\), \(\Delta=f(y)-f(x)\).
- Finite evaluation budget \(B\) (work units).

** What is the condition?
The law must be derived from the asymptotic model, not chosen as a free
cooling schedule. Multimodal *escape* is a different problem (outside this
unknown); here the condition is local *descent* of the chain state.

** Literature parent
Local Metropolis on a quadratic + ES progress theory + adaptive Metropolis
(Haario–Saksman–Tamminen) for proposal *shape*. This is *not* Xiang dual
annealing (GSA visiting + L-BFGS as a global solver).

* (II) Devise a plan

** Have we seen a related problem?
Yes: maximize expected progress of a \((1+1)\)-ES on the sphere (Rechenberg);
Metropolis at \(T=0\) recovers pure descent. Adaptive MCMC optimizes a
different objective (stationary diffusion speed, Roberts–Gelman–Gilks).

** Auxiliary problem
Pass to the \(d\to\infty\) limit with ES scaling so \(\Delta\) is Gaussian, then
optimize the normalized gain
\[
G(c,\theta)=-\mathbb{E}\bigl[D\min\{1,e^{-D/\theta}\}\bigr],\quad
\theta=Td/f(x),\quad D=d\Delta/f(x).
\]

** Tools for checking (not a methodology brand)
- SymPy: expand Gaussian density ratios and algebraic factors.
- Lean: machine-check the coefficient identities and \(\theta_\star\in(0,2)\).
- Implementation: enforce \(T=\theta_\star(f-f_{\mathrm{best}})/d\).

* (III) Carry out the plan

** Model (M1)
Under \(\sigma=c\|x\|/d\),
\[
D \;\Longrightarrow\; N(c^2,\,4c^2).
\]

** Identity (I1)
Let \(p_c\) be the \(N(c^2,4c^2)\) density. For \(u>0\),
\[
\frac{p_c(-u)}{p_c(u)}=e^{-u/2}.
\]
Proof sketch: \(\log p(-u)-\log p(u)=-2u\mu/\sigma^2\) for any Gaussian
\(N(\mu,\sigma^2)\); set \(\mu=c^2\), \(\sigma^2=4c^2\).

** Pairing (I2)–(T1)
\[
G(c,\theta)=\int_0^\infty u\,p_c(u)\,(e^{-u/2}-e^{-u/\theta})\,du.
\]
The weight \(e^{-u/2}-e^{-u/\theta}\) has the sign of \(2-\theta\) for \(u>0\).
Hence for every \(c>0\):
\[
G>0 \Leftrightarrow 0<\theta<2,\quad G(\cdot,2)=0,\quad G<0\Leftrightarrow\theta>2.
\]

** Operating point
Choose \(\theta_\star=\tfrac12\in(0,2)\). Numerics (SymPy/MC): at \(\theta_\star\),
\(c^\star\approx 1.32\), \(G^\star\approx 0.37\), \(\alpha^\star\approx 0.32\).

** Operating law (A1)
\[
T(x)=\theta_\star\frac{f(x)-f_{\mathrm{best}}}{d}=\frac12\frac{f(x)-f_{\mathrm{best}}}{d}.
\]
Proposal size: Robbins–Monro on \(\log\sigma\) targeting \(\alpha^\star=0.32\).
Proposal shape: running covariance when enough samples exist (else isotropic).
Budget: reserve a polish fraction \(\rho_{\mathrm{pol}}=\tfrac14\) of \(B\).

** Algorithm GPMD (explicit)

1. Initialize \(x\) (given \(x_0\) or random in the box); \(f\leftarrow f(x)\); \(f_{\mathrm{best}}\leftarrow f\).
2. \(B_{\mathrm{pol}}\leftarrow\lfloor B/4\rfloor\); \(B_{\mathrm{sa}}\leftarrow B-B_{\mathrm{pol}}\).
3. While work \(< B_{\mathrm{sa}}\):
   a. \(T\leftarrow \tfrac12\max(f-f_{\mathrm{best}},\varepsilon)/d\).
   b. Propose \(y\) from \(N(x,\sigma^2\hat\Sigma)\) (isotropic if scatter thin); reflect into box.
   c. Metropolis accept/reject with temperature \(T\); charge one objective eval.
   d. Update scatter / RM \(\log\sigma\) toward \(\alpha^\star=0.32\); update \(f_{\mathrm{best}}\).
4. Spend remaining polish budget on projected gradient (if gradient given) or coordinate polish of \(x_{\mathrm{best}}\).
5. Return \(x_{\mathrm{best}}\).

Parameters introduced above: \(\theta_\star=\tfrac12\), \(\alpha^\star=0.32\), \(\rho_{\mathrm{pol}}=\tfrac14\), \(\varepsilon>0\) floor on the gap. No further free cooling schedule.

* (IV) Look back

** Check the algebra
- SymPy: (I1), factor \(1/\theta-1/2=(2-\theta)/(2\theta)\), sign of \(G\) at \(\theta\in\{0.5,2,3\}\).
- Lean: coefficient \(2\mu/(4\mu)=1/2\); \(\theta_\star\in(0,2)\); factor trichotomy samples.

** Check the implementation
- Code must call \(T=\theta_\star(f-f_{\mathrm{best}})/d\) (not a geometric schedule).
- Unit tests: sphere improves under budget; fixed hot \(T\) loses on sphere; budget not exceeded.

** Can we use the result?
GPMD is a *local descent* arm. For deep multimodal barriers, pair with restarts
or a portfolio (escape window analysis). Do not claim global SOTA vs dual annealing
from (T1) alone.
